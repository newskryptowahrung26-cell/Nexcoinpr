How to Choose the Right Agency for Your Project

When evaluating crypto PR agencies, look beyond vanity metrics and ask the following 4 critical questions:

  1. Are placements guaranteed or pitched? Unearned pitching can burn thousands of dollars with zero published articles. Wires with guaranteed distribution ensure every dollar yields indexed links.
  2. Do you get permanent dofollow backlinks? Google rankings require high-authority, permanent dofollow links that do not get deleted 6 months later.
  3. Is there Wall Street / Financial syndication? Crypto projects need institutional trust. Ensure your provider covers mainstream financial terminals (Yahoo Finance, Benzinga, AP News).
  4. Are you locked into a multi-month contract? Avoid agencies requiring 3–6 month retainers unless you need dedicated crisis management.
