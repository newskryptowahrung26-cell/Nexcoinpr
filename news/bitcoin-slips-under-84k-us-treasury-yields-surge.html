Dogecoin Slides 8%, Bitcoin Drops Under $84,000 as Treasury Yields Hit 2007 Highs in Crypto Market Selloff

Dogecoin fell 8% and Bitcoin slid under $84,000 as surging US Treasury yields and $104 crude oil ignited a broad crypto market selloff.

By NexcoinPR Editorial Desk • Topic Source: CoinDesk Reporting → • Published: 24 September 2026

Source Citation & Editorial Note: This market report examines the crypto liquidations and macro drivers originally reported by CoinDesk, synthesized with on-chain order book depth, bond auction statistics, and derivatives positions reviewed by NexcoinPR.

1. Market Selloff Overview: Dogecoin Leads Token Declines Below Key Levels

Cryptocurrency markets faced sharp selling pressure on Thursday, with token liquidations spreading rapidly across major exchanges. Dogecoin (DOGE) suffered the steepest contraction among top-tier assets, tumbling between 7% and 8% to trade just above 9 cents ($0.09). The sudden move wiped out weekly accumulated gains and triggered cascading stop-loss orders across high-frequency derivatives desks.

Bitcoin (BTC) dropped beneath the $84,000 mark, breaking down through the $85,000 price level where substantial option positioning was concentrated. Other prominent digital currencies posted marked declines: Zcash (ZEC), Ripple (XRP), and Hyperliquid (HYPE) each shed 5% to 6% within hours. Meanwhile, Ether (ETH), Solana (SOL), and Binance Coin (BNB) retreated between 2% and 3%, while Tron (TRX) remained flat against the broader market decline.

Key Asset Declines and Price Points

  • Dogecoin (DOGE): Dropped 8% to hover just above 9 cents ($0.091).
  • Bitcoin (BTC): Slipped below $84,000, breaking through $85,000 call option strike bands.
  • Mid-Cap Tokens (ZEC, XRP, HYPE): Retreated between 5% and 6% across spot exchanges.
  • Large-Cap Tokens (ETH, SOL, BNB): Pulled back 2% to 3% during morning trading sessions.
  • Brent Crude Oil: Rebounded over 4% to nearly $104 per barrel.
  • 5-Year Treasury Note Auction Yield: Cleared at 5.033%, the highest auction yield since 2006.

2. The Macro Catalyst Trio: Oil Spike, Surging PMI, and Treasury Auction Weakness

The downturn across digital assets did not develop from protocol failures or crypto-specific regulatory actions. Instead, the selloff stemmed directly from a trio of macroeconomic shocks that pushed borrowing costs upward and unsettled global risk appetite.

First, energy commodities reversed direction abruptly. Brent crude oil prices rallied more than 4% to reach nearly $104 per barrel. This rally ended a six-session decline that had previously eased consumer price inflation anxieties, immediately reigniting concerns of persistent price pressures across global supply chains.

Second, economic growth indicators pointed to an overheating economy. The preliminary S&P Global flash survey of United States businesses revealed that domestic business output expanded at its fastest pace in over five years. The composite output index climbed to 58.4, marking its strongest performance since July 2021. Bitcoin recorded its steepest intraday price drop immediately following the release of this business survey data, as traders priced in a more restrictive policy environment from the Federal Reserve.

Third, fixed-income markets showed signs of strain during a sovereign debt sale. The United States Treasury conducted an auction of $70 billion in five-year notes, which met with weak primary dealer appetite. The auction cleared at a yield of 5.033%, marking the highest auction yield recorded since 2006. Crucially, the final yield landed approximately 3 basis points above secondary market trading levels immediately preceding the sale, confirming that institutional investors required additional compensation to absorb newly issued government paper.

3. Transmission Mechanism: Why Sovereign Yields Pressure Digital Assets

The rapid rise in sovereign yields creates a direct headwind for non-yielding assets such as Bitcoin and Dogecoin. When risk-free government obligations offer guaranteed returns exceeding 5%, the economic threshold for committing capital to volatile digital tokens rises significantly.

Furthermore, elevated interest rates elevate the capital expense required to finance leveraged trading positions. Margin borrowing rates across prime brokerages and centralized crypto lenders adjust upward in step with base rates. When funding costs rise while token prices stall, leveraged market participants face mounting incentives to close speculative trades and reduce balance-sheet exposure.

4. Derivatives Expiry and the $85,000 Call Option Threshold

Market depth and derivatives positioning magnified the price decline. With Bitcoin sliding under $84,000, the token has moved beneath the key $85,000 strike price highlighted by Mauricio Di Bartolomeo, co-founder of digital asset financial services firm Ledn.

Di Bartolomeo pointed out that a substantial block of call options had accumulated around the $85,000 level ahead of Friday's massive quarterly options settlement on the Deribit exchange. The upcoming expiry represents approximately $14 billion to $16 billion in open interest. When spot prices slide below concentrated call option strikes, option market makers who previously bought underlying spot Bitcoin to hedge delta exposure are forced to sell spot holdings into declining markets, deepening downside momentum.

5. Technical Support Levels and Order Book Depth

Technical chart patterns indicate that Bitcoin is now testing secondary defense zones between $82,500 and $83,200. A failure to hold this support range could expose the token to a deeper correction toward the round-number barrier at $80,000, where substantial institutional limit buy orders are positioned on Coinbase and Binance order books.

For Dogecoin, maintaining support above the 9-cent level ($0.090) remains essential to avoid technical breakdown toward 8 cents. Immediate resistance for Bitcoin now sits at $85,000, which has transitioned from former support into short-term overhead supply.

6. Corporate Communications for Crypto Firms During Market Selloffs

Periods of heightened market volatility test the communication strategies of Web3 foundations, fintech brokers, and decentralized protocols. When headlines spotlight double-digit token drawdowns and rising borrowing yields, media coverage tends to adopt defensive risk-off angles.

Web3 executive teams preparing corporate disclosures, token generation events, or funding announcements must adjust their communication posture. Emphasizing protocol utility, verified user growth, verified liquidity reserves, and transparent treasury practices enables companies to preserve trust among accredited investors and commercial partners.
