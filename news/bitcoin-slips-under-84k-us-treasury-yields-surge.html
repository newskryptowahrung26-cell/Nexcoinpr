Dogecoin Slides 8%, Bitcoin Drops Under $84,000 as Treasury Yields Hit 2007 Highs in Crypto Market Selloff

Dogecoin fell 8% and Bitcoin slid under $84,000 as surging US Treasury yields and $104 crude oil ignited a broad crypto market selloff.

By NexcoinPR Editorial Desk • ✍️ Human-Researched & Fact-Checked • Topic Source: CoinDesk Reporting → • Published: 24 September 2026

Source Citation & Editorial Note: This market report examines the crypto liquidations and macro drivers originally reported by CoinDesk, synthesized with on-chain order book depth, bond auction statistics, and derivatives positions reviewed by NexcoinPR.

Quick Market Takeaway: Cryptocurrency markets declined sharply as Dogecoin slid 8% and Bitcoin dropped below $84,000 following a multi-year surge in US Treasury auction yields to 5.033% and a sudden rally in crude oil prices toward $104 per barrel.

1. Market Selloff Overview: Dogecoin Leads Token Declines Below Key Levels

Cryptocurrency markets faced sharp selling pressure on Thursday, with token liquidations spreading rapidly across major exchanges. Dogecoin (DOGE) suffered the steepest contraction among top-tier assets, tumbling between 7% and 8% to trade just above 9 cents ($0.09). The sudden move wiped out weekly accumulated gains and triggered cascading stop-loss orders across high-frequency derivatives desks.

Bitcoin (BTC) dropped beneath the $84,000 mark, breaking down through the $85,000 price level where substantial option positioning was concentrated. Other prominent digital currencies posted marked declines: Zcash (ZEC), Ripple (XRP), and Hyperliquid (HYPE) each shed 5% to 6% within hours. Meanwhile, Ether (ETH), Solana (SOL), and Binance Coin (BNB) retreated between 2% and 3%, while Tron (TRX) remained flat against the broader market decline.

Key Asset Declines and Price Points

  • Dogecoin (DOGE): Dropped 8% to hover just above 9 cents ($0.091).
  • Bitcoin (BTC): Slipped below $84,000, breaking through $85,000 call option strike bands.
  • Mid-Cap Tokens (ZEC, XRP, HYPE): Retreated between 5% and 6% across spot exchanges.
  • Large-Cap Tokens (ETH, SOL, BNB): Pulled back 2% to 3% during morning trading sessions.
  • Brent Crude Oil: Rebounded over 4% to nearly $104 per barrel.
  • 5-Year Treasury Note Auction Yield: Cleared at 5.033%, the highest auction yield since 2006.

2. The Macro Catalyst Trio: Oil Spike, Surging PMI, and Treasury Auction Weakness

The downturn across digital assets stemmed from three macroeconomic catalysts that pushed borrowing costs upward and unsettled global risk appetite.

First, energy commodities reversed direction abruptly as Brent crude oil rallied over 4% toward $104 per barrel, reigniting concerns of persistent inflation across global supply chains. Second, the preliminary S&P Global flash survey showed United States business output expanded at its fastest pace in five years with a composite index of 58.4, leading traders to price in a more restrictive policy stance from the Federal Reserve. Third, the United States Treasury's $70 billion auction of five-year notes cleared at 5.033%, marking the highest auction yield recorded since 2006 and confirming that investors required elevated yields to absorb sovereign debt.

3. Transmission Mechanism: Why Sovereign Yields Pressure Digital Assets

The rapid climb in sovereign yields creates a headwind for non-yielding assets like Bitcoin and Dogecoin. When risk-free government paper offers yields exceeding 5%, the hurdle rate for holding volatile digital tokens increases substantially.

Higher interest rates also increase financing costs for leveraged positions. Margin borrowing rates across prime brokerages adjust upward alongside base rates, prompting traders to close speculative trades and reduce balance-sheet exposure.

4. Derivatives Expiry and the $85,000 Call Option Threshold

Derivatives positioning accelerated the decline as Bitcoin dropped beneath $84,000, falling below the key $85,000 strike price highlighted by Mauricio Di Bartolomeo, co-founder of Ledn. Di Bartolomeo noted that a large volume of call options was concentrated around $85,000 ahead of quarterly options settlement on Deribit. When spot prices broke below this level, option market makers hedging delta were compelled to sell spot Bitcoin, amplifying downside momentum.

5. Technical Support Levels and Order Book Depth

Technical chart patterns indicate that Bitcoin is now testing secondary defense zones between $82,500 and $83,200. A failure to hold this support range could expose the token to a deeper correction toward the round-number barrier at $80,000, where substantial institutional limit buy orders are positioned on Coinbase and Binance order books.

For Dogecoin, maintaining support above the 9-cent level ($0.090) remains essential to avoid technical breakdown toward 8 cents. Immediate resistance for Bitcoin now sits at $85,000, which has transitioned from former support into short-term overhead supply.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

What triggered the sharp downturn in Bitcoin and Dogecoin?

The downturn was ignited by macroeconomic catalysts including a 4% spike in crude oil to nearly $104 per barrel, a five-year high in US business activity surveys, and weak demand at a $70 billion Treasury auction clearing at 5.033%.

Why did Bitcoin fall rapidly once it broke under $85,000?

The $85,000 price level represented a concentrated cluster of call option strikes ahead of quarterly expiry. When spot prices broke below this threshold, market makers sold spot holdings to dynamically hedge delta exposure, accelerating the decline.
