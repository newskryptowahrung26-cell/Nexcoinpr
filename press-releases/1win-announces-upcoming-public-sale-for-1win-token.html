1win Announces Upcoming Public Sale for $1WIN Token

Issuer: 1win • Source: Decrypt → • Published: 13 October 2026

Willemstad, Curaçao, October 13th, 2026

1win, a crypto entertainment platform, has announced the upcoming public sale of $1WIN, a new Solana-based token designed to extend the company’s existing gaming ecosystem with Web3 utility, user rewards, and revenue-linked token mechanics. The token sale starts here on October 15th at 4 pm BST.

The public sale marks the next stage in the rollout of $1WIN, which is being introduced to a platform serving 100 million registered players across international markets. Unlike token projects built around a future product roadmap, 1win’s existing business operates independently of the token and already generates revenue through its gaming and entertainment products.

$1WIN will have a fixed total supply of 1 billion tokens, with no additional minting after TGE. The token is being developed for use across 1win products, including gaming, lottery, rewards, and other ecosystem functions.

Two mechanisms will connect the token directly with activity on the existing platform: open-market buybacks funded by gaming revenue and the permanent burning of 10% of $1WIN spent through supported platform products.

Rather than financing buybacks from a finite pool of token reserves, 1win plans to fund regular $1WIN purchases using revenue generated by its existing gaming business. Separately, 10% of $1WIN spent through supported products will be permanently removed from supply through a smart contract.

The majority of the token supply is also allocated around user participation. 51% of the total supply is designated for user-facing mechanisms, including 25% for platform game reward pools, 18% for Wager Rewards, and 8% for distribution through the 1win Telegram Mini App.

$1WIN will be issued as a native token on Solana, allowing it to integrate with standard Solana wallets and decentralized exchanges alongside its planned use within the 1win ecosystem. The upcoming public sale will provide the first broader opportunity for users to access $1WIN ahead of its wider ecosystem rollout.

The token forms part of 1win’s broader expansion into Web3, and its structure is designed to make the token an extension of an operating business. The platform already supports multi-chain cryptocurrency deposits and withdrawals and introduced wallet-based authentication through Trust Wallet, MetaMask, and WalletConnect earlier this year, allowing users to register, sign in, and deposit through a connected crypto wallet.

About 1win 

Founded in 2016, 1win is a crypto entertainment platform in the global gaming industry. Operating across Asia, Latin America, and Africa, 1win offers a wide range of entertainment products adapted to regional audiences. The brand collaborates with international public figures, including UFC legend Ilia Topuria, Olympic champion and UFC fighter Gable Steveson, professional boxer Ryan Garcia, rapper Tyga, reggaeton star Nicky Jam, and UFC interviewer Nina Drama, all of whom are 1win VIP community members. 1win also partners with the football legend Luis Suárez.
