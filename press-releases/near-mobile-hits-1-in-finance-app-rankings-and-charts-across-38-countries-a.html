NEAR Mobile Hits #1 in Finance App Rankings and Charts Across 38 Countries as NEAR Ecosystem Gains Momentum

Escaldes-Engordany, Andorra, November 4th, 2026

With over 1 million downloads, the NEAR ecosystem's largest mobile wallet expands its global reach while launching gasless cross-chain swaps across 120+ assets and 35+ blockchains powered by NEAR Intents.

NEAR Mobile is gaining international traction, appearing in App Store rankings across 38 countries over a recent 31-day period, according to app ranking data from Toplify.

The self-custodial wallet reached #1 in Finance in Serbia, #2 in Hungary, and #4 in Moldova and Oman, highlighting its growing visibility across international markets. NEAR Mobile also recently reached #7 in Finance in Finland and Nigeria.

These milestones come amid growing attention around the NEAR ecosystem and its chain abstraction infrastructure. NEAR Intents, the protocol powering cross-chain execution across the ecosystem, has surpassed $33 billion in cumulative volume across 35 blockchains.

With more than one million downloads, NEAR Mobile is the largest mobile wallet in the NEAR ecosystem and continues to expand its capabilities with version 4.0.0, introducing a gasless experience designed to make blockchain interactions significantly simpler.

From Global Adoption to a Gasless Multichain Experience

As NEAR Mobile expands its international reach, its latest update addresses one of crypto's biggest usability challenges: gas fees.

Traditionally, users interacting with multiple blockchains must maintain different native tokens to pay transaction fees, even when they already hold the assets they want to transfer or swap.

With version 4.0.0, NEAR Mobile sponsors relayer fees for supported transactions, enabling users to swap and move assets across supported networks without needing to hold NEAR or maintain native gas tokens across different blockchains.

Powered by NEAR Intents, NEAR Mobile provides access to more than 120 assets across 35+ blockchains, combining cross-chain execution, unified balances, staking and self-custodial asset management in a single mobile interface.

One Wallet. All Chains. Powered by NEAR.

Unified Balances Across 35+ Blockchains

Beyond removing gas complexity, NEAR Mobile addresses another major challenge in crypto: fragmented liquidity.

A user holding USDC across Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain and TRON traditionally has separate balances that may require manual bridging and network switching to use together.

Through NEAR Intents, NEAR Mobile can present supported liquidity from multiple networks as a unified balance, allowing users to send or swap aggregated assets without manually bridging funds first.

Cross-chain swaps can settle in seconds, bringing the experience closer to using a single financial account rather than navigating multiple independent blockchains.

By abstracting networks, bridges and gas tokens, NEAR Mobile makes NEAR's chain abstraction technology accessible to everyday users.

The Largest Mobile Wallet in the NEAR Ecosystem

With over one million downloads and growing visibility across international Finance App Store rankings, NEAR Mobile has established itself as a leading consumer application within the NEAR ecosystem.

Available on both iOS and Android, the self-custodial NEAR wallet allows users to create and manage NEAR accounts, send and receive assets, stake NEAR and access multichain functionality directly from their phones.

NEAR Mobile supports onboarding through Google, Apple ID or email, combining a familiar mobile experience with self-custody and on-chain account recovery.

Experienced users can alternatively create or import wallets using a traditional mnemonic phrase.

The wallet also provides NEAR staking and access to NPRO staking, currently offering up to 8.58% APY.

As NEAR's chain abstraction ecosystem continues expanding, NEAR Mobile aims to make its infrastructure accessible through an intuitive consumer-facing application.

About NEAR Mobile

NEAR Mobile is the largest self-custodial mobile wallet in the NEAR ecosystem, with more than 1 million downloads across iOS and Android.

Powered by NEAR Intents, the wallet enables users to manage NEAR, stake assets and access more than 120 tokens across 35+ blockchains through gasless cross-chain functionality and unified balances.

Its mission is to make NEAR's chain abstraction infrastructure accessible to everyone by removing the complexity of networks, bridges and gas tokens.
