PrimeXBT Celebrates 8 Years of Redefining Crypto Trading with 50% Deposit Bonus

Castries, Saint Lucia, November 26th, 2026

PrimeXBT celebrates its 8th anniversary, marking its evolution from crypto margin trading into a global multi-asset broker and crypto asset service provider. Since its founding in 2018, the company has expanded its offering through continuous product innovation and professional-grade technology, building a global community of over one million traders across more than 150 countries.

To celebrate this milestone and recognise the continued trust and support throughout its journey, PrimeXBT now offers a 50% Deposit Bonus* (T&Cs apply). Available on both MT5 and PXTrader 2.0, the bonus can be activated through the Rewards Center under My Promo, giving clients additional trading margin and greater flexibility to manage their positions and trading strategies.

Building on its crypto trading foundations, PrimeXBT has developed an innovative multi-asset ecosystem that connects crypto and traditional financial markets in one place. It brings together broker's flagship PXTrader 2.0 platform with over 350 trading instruments across Crypto Futures and CFDs, MetaTrader 5, an integrated crypto exchange and a secure wallet with support for over 50 cryptocurrencies across multiple blockchain networks. Clients can buy, store and exchange digital assets, and use their crypto holdings as trading capital to access Forex, commodities, indices and shares without converting them into fiat.

In 2026, PrimeXBT further expanded opportunities for crypto traders, introducing Tokenised Stock Crypto Futures, Pre-IPO Trading, and 24/7 Gold trading. Reflecting the growing demand for advanced trading tools as the crypto industry matures, the broker integrated with TradingView, combining professional-grade charting and market analysis with direct trade execution. It also introduced Model Context Protocol (MCP) connectivity and a new Trading API to enable AI-assisted account interaction and trading automation. 

Beyond product innovation, PrimeXBT has focused on delivering top-tier trading conditions and greater value for clients, offering low fees, Swap-Free options and additional cost savings through its VIP Tiers Program and Rewards Center, reflecting its continued commitment to putting traders first.

Jonatan Randin, CMT, Senior Market Analyst at PrimeXBT, commented: "Eight years ago, we set out to challenge the way people access and trade financial markets. Since then, we've continuously pushed forward with new technology, improved trading conditions and solutions designed around traders' needs. Our growth reflects that focus, but we're not standing still. As the industry evolves, our ambition is to stay ahead of the curve, keep raising standards and deliver a superior trading experience for clients worldwide."

PrimeXBT's journey has been supported by continued international expansion and a growing regulatory footprint, including regulation by South Africa's Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA). The company has also strengthened its presence through local partnerships, educational initiatives and community engagement across Africa, Asia and Latin America, while earning over 30 industry awards for its trading platforms, conditions and services.

As PrimeXBT enters its ninth year, it remains focused on expanding market access, advancing its trading technology and strengthening the connection between crypto and traditional finance. With AI-assisted trading and new market opportunities becoming part of its ecosystem, the company continues to build on its progress, driving innovation and shaping the future of trading.

About PrimeXBT

PrimeXBT is a global multi-asset broker and crypto asset service provider trusted by traders in more than 150 countries. The platform bridges traditional and digital markets within one integrated environment, redefining versatility and innovation in online trading. Clients can access Forex, CFDs on indices, commodities, shares, crypto, and Crypto Futures, as well as buy, store and exchange cryptocurrencies. This unified experience extends across the native PXTrader 2.0 platform, MetaTrader 5, and the TradingView platform, supported by advanced risk-management tools and a wide range of funding options in crypto, fiat and local payment methods. Since 2018, PrimeXBT has focused on empowering traders through broad multi-asset access, fair and transparent conditions, professional-grade technology and dedicated human support. By combining expertise, trust and a client-first approach, PrimeXBT sets a benchmark of excellence in the financial industry and provides traders with the tools they need to trade, grow and succeed with confidence.
