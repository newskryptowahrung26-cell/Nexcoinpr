Capital Group Quantitative Investments LLC Highlights Three AI Products for Multi-Asset Quantitative Finance

New York, NY, November 17th, 2026

Capital Group Quantitative Investments LLC (CGQI) offers three multi-asset fintech products designed to address different needs in quantitative finance: CGQI One, Capital Algo and Capital Quant.

The company’s product portfolio covers several areas of quantitative financial technology, including market data analysis, algorithmic trading, quantitative modelling and investment strategy research.

CGQI One combines market data with AI-generated strategies for stocks and other financial assets. Capital Algo focuses on algorithmic trading, quantitative models and order routing, while Capital Quant provides tools for factor analysis, backtesting and strategy research.

Together, the three products represent different components of the company’s approach to AI-powered quantitative finance. Their respective functions address market analysis, algorithmic execution and research-oriented strategy development.

Capital Group Quantitative Investments LLC lists 2020 as its founding year. Its stated areas of focus include artificial intelligence, quantitative algorithms, financial data analysis and financial technology services across multiple asset classes.

Three Core Products

CGQI One: An integrated, multi-asset AI system for quantitative finance. It provides in-depth market data for stocks and other financial assets, AI-powered strategy generation, multi-asset data analysis and intelligent asset management tools. It aims to improve the efficiency of financial data processing and the user experience.

Capital Algo: An algorithm-driven financial technology system. It focuses on algorithmic analysis, quantitative model optimization and research into order-routing technology. It is designed for users who prioritize algorithm efficiency, market liquidity analysis and cost optimization.

Capital Quant: An AI quantitative research system for data analysis and strategy development. It combines multidimensional financial data, AI-based factor analysis, historical strategy backtesting and quantitative model research. Users can build, test and optimize their own quantitative strategies.

Company Development Priorities

Capital Group Quantitative Investments LLC focuses on artificial intelligence and financial technology innovation. It aims to bring together technologies used in traditional securities markets and the financial technology sector.

Through CGQI One, Capital Algo and Capital Quant, the company focuses on AI-powered multi-asset analysis, financial algorithm optimization and quantitative strategy research. It continues to explore how AI can make financial technology services more efficient and transparent.

About Capital Group Quantitative Investments LLC

Capital Group Quantitative Investments LLC develops financial technology that uses artificial intelligence and quantitative methods to analyze stocks and financial assets. CGQI One combines stock and financial market data with tools for generating strategies. Capital Algo focuses on quantitative model optimization, market liquidity analysis and order-routing research. Capital Quant helps users analyze factors, test strategies against historical data and refine their own quantitative models.
