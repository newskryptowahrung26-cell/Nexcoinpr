ChangeNOW Becomes the Exclusive Bitcoin Swap Provider in CoolWallet

Kingstown, St. Vincent & the Grenadines, November 11th, 2026 Every Bitcoin swap a CoolWallet user makes in the app, whether BTC is...

Issuer: ChangeNOW • Source: Decrypt → • Published: 11 November 2026

Commercial Content Disclosure: The following announcement is an official press release syndicated from Decrypt on behalf of ChangeNOW. NexcoinPR provides media distribution and editorial hosting. This content does not represent independent editorial reporting or investment advice.

Kingstown, St. Vincent & the Grenadines, November 11th, 2026

Every Bitcoin swap a CoolWallet user makes in the app, whether BTC is sent or received, now runs through ChangeNOW.

ChangeNOW, a crypto super app for businesses, is now the exclusive swap provider for BTC-related pairs in the CoolWallet app. Any in-app swap where a user sends or receives Bitcoin is processed by ChangeNOW.

This is the next step in the partnership after the co-branded hardware wallets the two companies released at TOKEN2049 Singapore in October.

BTC Swaps in the CoolWallet App

Swaps such as BTC to USDT and ETH to BTC both fall under the exclusivity, which runs from November 10, 2026, to January 10, 2027. It covers the standard CoolWallet Pro and CoolWallet Go.

Users see no change in the app. A user picks the assets on the swap screen and confirms the transaction on the CoolWallet device. ChangeNOW is non-custodial and requires no registration.

On ChangeNOW you can swap Bitcoin for any of the 1,300+ assets. Which of those pairs you can open in CoolWallet is going to be down to which networks the app supports.

How the Partnership Got Here

CoolWallet integrated the ChangeNOW Exchange API as an in-app swap provider on July 30, 2026. From October 9 to November 9, 2026, ChangeNOW was the default swap provider in the app.

Co-Branded Hardware Wallets from TOKEN2049 Singapore

In October, CoolWallet and ChangeNOW released a limited run of co-branded hardware wallets at TOKEN2049 Singapore, held at Marina Bay Sands on October 7-8. Visitors received the devices through activities at the ChangeNOW booth.

Each device carries a custom design and has ChangeNOW built in as its only in-app swap provider. Users of the co-branded wallets already swap every pair through ChangeNOW, including pairs without Bitcoin.

About CoolWallet

CoolWallet have been making hardware wallets since 2014 and currently have over 300k users. The CoolWallet Pro and the NFC enabled CoolWallet Go both use a credit card sized device in conjunction with the CoolWallet app which manages both cold and hot storage. The private keys are generated on the secure chip in the device and never leave it. The app supports buying, staking and swapping across Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, EVM chains and other networks.

Website: CoolWallet.io

About ChangeNOW

ChangeNOW For Business is an enterprise-focused crypto super app that gives companies secure tools to exchange and manage digital assets, accept payments, and integrate Web3 finance into their operations.

The platform covers 1,300+ coins across 90+ networks and over 2.25 million exchange pairs, with liquidity coming from both CEXs and DEXs. Wallets integrate through the Crypto Exchange API and earn from 0.4% on every transaction. SOC 2 and ISO 27001 practices back dozens of millions of transactions a month for partners in fintech, iGaming, lending and investment.

Website: https://changenow.io/for-partners
