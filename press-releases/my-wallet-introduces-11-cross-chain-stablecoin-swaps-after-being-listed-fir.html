My Wallet Introduces 1:1 Cross-Chain Stablecoin Swaps After Being Listed First on WalletConnect

Dubai, United Arab Emirates, November 19th, 2026 My Wallet users can now receive a different stablecoin on another blockchain at a fixed...

Issuer: My Wallet • Source: Decrypt → • Published: 19 November 2026

Commercial Content Disclosure: The following announcement is an official press release syndicated from Decrypt on behalf of My Wallet. NexcoinPR provides media distribution and editorial hosting. This content does not represent independent editorial reporting or investment advice.

Dubai, United Arab Emirates, November 19th, 2026

My Wallet users can now receive a different stablecoin on another blockchain at a fixed 1:1 token rate on eligible routes, with no spread, slippage or swap fee charged by the wallet

My Wallet, a self-custodial, open-source crypto wallet serving more than 10 million users, has launched 1:1 swaps for USDT, USDC, USDe and USDS across supported blockchains. On eligible routes, users can switch both stablecoins and networks within a single in-app flow, with the amount they will receive fixed before confirmation. 

The launch follows My Wallet’s listing at the top of WalletConnect’s WalletGuide. The wallet is also WalletConnect Certified and connects users to more than 80,000 compatible apps.

Simplifying Stablecoin Transfers Across Blockchains

According to research published in October 2026 by Coinbase and The Block Pro Research, organic stablecoin transfer volume exceeded $400 million per hour during Q3 2026. Yet stablecoins operate across multiple blockchains and token issuers, creating friction when users need a specific token on a specific network. Even when funds arrive on the right network, users may still hold the wrong stablecoin for a payment, trade or application.

My Wallet addresses this through its in-app swap aggregator, powered by LayerZero, NEAR Intents, and other bridges resulting in the best rate. On eligible stablecoin routes, the aggregator offers a fixed 1:1 token rate, with no exchange-rate spread, slippage or My Wallet swap commission. My Wallet is the first among major crypto wallets to offer 1:1 swaps between different stablecoins across blockchains in a single in-app flow.

How the 1:1 Stablecoin Swaps Work

The fixed 1:1 rate can apply whether users switch to a different stablecoin or move the same token to another blockchain. On a supported cross-chain USDT-to-USDC route, for example, a user sending 10,000 USDT receives exactly 10,000 USDC. The same rate applies when the token stays the same: a supported transfer of 10,000 USDT from Ethereum to TRON delivers exactly 10,000 USDT on TRON.

The 1:1 option is available for swaps above $30 on supported routes. There is no exchange-rate spread, slippage or My Wallet swap commission. Applicable blockchain network fees are paid separately, and the exact destination token amount is shown before confirmation. Other routes may have different rates and fees.

“Stablecoins are supposed to make moving money simpler, but users still have to think about which token they hold and which blockchain it’s on,” said Alexander Zinchuk, founder of My Wallet. “We’re removing that friction. If you hold USDT and need USDC on another network, you should be able to make that change in one step, know exactly how many tokens you’ll receive, and not pay an additional swap commission to your wallet.”

Native Bitcoin and Gasless EVM Transfers

The stablecoin launch follows the introduction of native Bitcoin support in My Wallet. Users can hold, send, receive and swap BTC alongside assets on Ethereum, Solana, TON and other networks, as well as buy Bitcoin with a bank card or sell it for fiat through MoonPay.

My Wallet now supports gasless transfers on Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum and Optimism, adding to the existing support on Solana and TON. For these EVM transfers, the network fee is deducted from the tokens being sent, so users do not need to acquire a separate native token for gas. Supported assets are USDC on all five EVM networks, USDT on Ethereum and BNB Chain, bridged USDT on Base and Optimism, and USDT0 on Arbitrum. There is no minimum transfer amount or daily limit on the number of transactions.

Together, these innovative features advance My Wallet’s broader “All You Need to Enjoy Crypto” vision, combining asset management, frictionless trading, and ease of use into one self-custodial app.

The new features are already available in My Wallet. Swap availability is subject to the selected assets, networks and routes.

About My Wallet

My Wallet is a self-custodial, open-source multichain wallet for Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Hyperliquid, TON, TRON, BNB, Base, Robinhood, Monad, Arbitrum, Polygon, and more. Founded in 2022, My Wallet serves over 10 million users across mobile, desktop, web and its Telegram Mini App. The wallet has been audited by CertiK and ranks among the top-5 wallets on CertiK’s Wallet Leaderboard.

My Wallet is WalletConnect Certified and currently listed 1st in WalletConnect’s WalletGuide, connecting users to more than 80,000 compatible apps.

Staking inside My Wallet is powered by Stakee, its own staking protocol, ranked among the top-2 TON DeFi protocols by TVL on DefiLlama.

For more details, users can visit mywallet.io and try My Wallet at mywallet.io/get.
