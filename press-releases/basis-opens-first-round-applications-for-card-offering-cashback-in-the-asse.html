BASIS Opens First-Round Applications for Card Offering Cashback in the Asset Spent

Victoria, Seychelles, November 27th, 2026 Available in Standard and Platinum editions, the BASIS Card offers up to 10% cashback, with...

Victoria, Seychelles, November 27th, 2026

Available in Standard and Platinum editions, the BASIS Card offers up to 10% cashback, with applicants selecting their preferred tier subject to staking eligibility

The BASIS Card returns money differently. Announced today by BASIS DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE LTD, the payment card credits cashback in the asset used for each eligible purchase, with Platinum cardholders qualifying for 10% cashback during the introductory Launch Boost period.

For digital asset holders, the principle is straightforward. Spend Bitcoin and receive Bitcoin back. Pay with Ethereum and receive Ethereum back. There are no reward points to redeem or proprietary platform tokens to manage. Cashback is credited in an asset the cardholder already uses, giving everyday spending a direct connection to their digital asset holdings.

The opening of first-round applications marks an expansion of BASIS's existing digital asset infrastructure into everyday payments. Applications are submitted at https://basis.pro/card and close on December 31, 2026, at 23:59 UTC.

Two Card Editions With Different Levels of Benefits

The BASIS Card is available in Standard, finished in matte black composite, and Platinum, produced in metal. Applicants select their preferred edition when submitting their waitlist application, with qualification determined by their active staking position.

Each edition has a separate staking eligibility threshold, as detailed below. BASIS measures the value of the applicant's active staking position against the requirement for their selected tier. The position is assessed at submission and re-checked at the close of the application period.

Participants can therefore review the benefits of each edition alongside its staking requirements before deciding which card to apply for. Final issuance remains subject to the issuing partner's independent review.

Card Fees, Cashback, and Launch Boost Benefits

Both editions feature an introductory Launch Boost period, beginning on the date of card issuance. During this period, cardholders receive enhanced cashback rates, higher monthly reward caps, and additional subscription benefits.

*All monetary figures are expressed in USD. Physical card issuance fees include applicable shipping charges.

Launch Boost runs for three months following issuance, after which each card returns to its respective base benefits. Standard and Platinum also feature rate-lock periods of 12 and 24 months, respectively, subject to the applicable program terms.

Along with cashback, the card program brings together subscription rebates of 50% or 100%, depending on the edition and benefit period, across a catalog of over 80 subscriptions. Travel and additional card benefits include two annual airport lounge visits, a 3 GB eSIM, and one additional card, subject to tier eligibility and applicable terms. Standard cardholders benefit from a 2% FX fee, while Platinum cardholders pay no FX fee during Launch Boost. Both editions provide immediate access to a virtual card upon activation, with Platinum participants also receiving priority and dedicated support.

Paid Back in the Asset You Spent

The defining feature of the BASIS Card is what happens after a purchase. Where conventional reward programs may require cardholders to accumulate points or convert rewards into their preferred currency, BASIS keeps the cashback denomination consistent with the asset spent.

Cashback on eligible settled purchases is credited directly to the cardholder's Funding Wallet. A purchase made using BTC returns BTC, leaving the recipient free to decide how to use the reward.

Cardholders who wish to stake their cashback can do so separately under the platform's ordinary conversion rules. Cashback does not automatically enter an existing staking position, and card rewards remain independent of staking yield.

Submitting a card application also leaves existing staking positions, lock-up periods, Dynamic Reward Rates, boosters, and stToken balances unchanged.

First-Round Applications and Card Issuance

Applications are free and limited to one per account during Round 01. Participants must hold at least USD 1,000 in eligible BASIS wallet value at submission, maintain an account in good standing, and satisfy the staking requirements for their selected card edition.

Once BASIS approves a waitlist application, the applicant receives further instructions and a secure Know Your Customer (KYC) verification link. The issuing partner then conducts its own review, covering identity verification, residency, regional eligibility, sanctions screening, and supporting documentation.

Waitlist approval permits applicants to proceed to the next stage and does not constitute final card approval, activation, or issuance.

Further information on card eligibility, benefits, fees, and application requirements is available in the official BASIS Card Program documentation.

About BASIS

BASIS DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE LTD is a Seychelles international business company (LEI: 254900IX2F2KCWNSSS64) operating market-neutral yield infrastructure, including execution, risk-management, and settlement systems for digital asset staking.

The company holds ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 certification, both verifiable through IAF CertSearch.

Technical documentation is available at docs.basis.pro.

Base cashback rates are fixed for 12 months on Standard and 24 months on Platinum. Launch Boost rates may be adjusted after the boost period with 30 days' notice. Both are separate from platform staking rewards, which vary with market conditions.
