Omer Istanbul Dental Clinic Moves into One of Europe’s Largest Dental Complexes

Istanbul, Türkiye, October 15th, 2026

The new 5,200-square-meter, seven-storey facility in Istanbul brings together advanced digital dentistry, expanded surgical capabilities, patient-centered architecture and dedicated international patient care services.

ISTANBUL, Türkiye, October 15, 2026 (EZ Newswire) — Responding to growing demand from international patients, Omer Istanbul Dental Clinic has officially relocated to a new 5,200-square-metre, seven-storey multidisciplinary dental complex near Istanbul’s prestigious Florya area. The move marks a major milestone in the clinic’s expansion of clinical capacity, digital infrastructure and international patient services.

The purpose-built facility features 33 treatment and procedure rooms, advanced surgical infrastructure, fully integrated digital clinical workflows and dedicated patient support teams, bringing multidisciplinary dental services together under one roof.

“Our move is about much more than creating a larger clinical space. We wanted to build an environment where advanced technology, multidisciplinary expertise and patient care could work together more effectively,” said Dr. Omer Karaaslan, founder and Chief Dentist of Omer Istanbul Dental Clinic. “For patients travelling internationally, the experience extends well beyond the treatment itself. This investment allows us to strengthen every stage of that journey, including the support we provide after they return home.”

Fully Digital Dentistry and Advanced Surgical Capabilities

At the heart of the new complex is a fully integrated, 100% digital dentistry workflow designed to support greater precision, efficiency and patient comfort throughout the treatment process.

By connecting every stage of care, from initial diagnostics and treatment planning to digitally guided procedures and restorative solutions, the system enables multidisciplinary teams to collaborate more effectively, minimise manual processes and reduce the potential for human error.

Combined with advanced surgical infrastructure, this digital-first approach supports complex treatments, including dental implants, aesthetic and restorative dentistry, oral surgery and full-mouth rehabilitation, while creating a more streamlined and comfortable experience for patients.

Architecture Designed Around Patient Comfort

Beyond its clinical capabilities, the new facility has been thoughtfully designed to create a welcoming and comfortable environment for patients.

Spacious waiting lounges, dedicated relaxation areas and carefully planned interior spaces are intended to offer a calmer experience throughout the treatment journey. The architectural approach combines the functionality of a modern healthcare facility with an emphasis on comfort, privacy and patient well-being.

“We wanted to create a space that feels different from a traditional dental clinic. From spacious waiting lounges to dedicated relaxation areas, every detail was considered to create a calm, welcoming environment where patients can feel comfortable and at ease throughout their visit,” added Dr. Karaaslan.

Dedicated International Patient Experience and After Care

Recognising the additional coordination involved in travelling abroad for dental treatment, Omer Istanbul Dental Clinic has strengthened its international patient services through two dedicated teams.

The Patient Experience team supports patients throughout their stay in Istanbul, coordinating appointments, communication and practical arrangements related to their treatment.

The After Care team provides post-treatment follow-up, ongoing communication and assistance after patients return to their home countries, extending the clinic’s patient support beyond the treatment itself.

These services are complemented by multilingual patient coordinators and full-scale support throughout the international patient journey, from travel and accommodation arrangements to treatment coordination.

A Strategic Investment in International Dental Care

The relocation represents a long-term investment in clinical infrastructure, digital technology and a more integrated approach to international patient care.

By bringing multidisciplinary dental services, advanced surgical capabilities, patient-centred spaces and dedicated support teams together under one roof, the new complex is designed to support the clinic’s continued growth while strengthening the overall patient experience.

About Omer Istanbul Dental Clinic

Omer Istanbul Dental Clinic is an internationally recognised dental healthcare provider based in Istanbul, Türkiye, founded by Dr. Omer Karaaslan. Named “International Cosmetic Dental Clinic of the Year,” the clinic has treated thousands of patients from more than 80 countries, specialising in aesthetic dentistry, dental implantology and full-scale oral rehabilitation. Operating from a new 5,200-square-metre, 7-storey multidisciplinary dental complex, the clinic combines advanced digital dentistry, surgical expertise and patient-centred care. Its services include smile design, zirconia crowns, E-max veneers, dental implants, full-mouth restorations and advanced surgical procedures. Certified by Türkiye’s Ministry of Health for international health tourism, Omer Istanbul Dental Clinic offers multilingual patient coordination, full-scale travel and treatment support, and dedicated Patient Experience and After Care services for patients from around the world. For more information, visit omeristanbul.com.
