More Than a Swap: PancakeSwap Rebrands as Pancake Ahead of Mobile App Launch

Singapore, Singapore, November 18th, 2026

PancakeSwap today announced that it has rebranded as Pancake. The new name reflects how far the platform has grown since launch, from a single-chain token swap into a multichain liquidity hub where users can trade, earn and build. 

Pancake launched in 2020 on one chain, known for one thing: swapping tokens. Over six years, it expanded to 11+ chains including BNB Chain, Base, Robinhood Chain, Arbitrum, Solana and Ethereum, and added products well beyond swaps. More than 208 million users have traded over $4.4 trillion on the platform.

Today, Pancake brings three groups of products together in one place:

  • Trade: Swap any token, go long or short with Perps or buy tokenized stocks and ETFs 24/7 on Stock Terminal, weekends included. The Pancake Aggregator routes each trade to the best available price across the market.
  • Earn: Stake CAKE in Syrup Pools or use Dual Investment to set a target price and earn yield while waiting for it.
  • Build: Developers can add custom features to Pancake's liquidity pools through Infinity hooks or integrate the Pancake Aggregator directly into their own apps.

Upcoming Mobile App Launch

A new name is only the first course. In the coming weeks, Pancake will launch its mobile app, built to be the best way to trade DeFi from your pocket: the full Pancake stack, one tap away, around the clock. Early users can expect a few surprises along the way so stay tuned.

About Pancake

Pancake (formerly PancakeSwap) is a multichain liquidity hub live across 11+ chains. Users can swap, trade perpetuals and tokenized stocks, and earn with Syrup Pools and Dual Investment. Builders can tap Pancake's liquidity through Infinity, its DEX aggregator and B2B integrations. Launched in 2020, Pancake has processed over $4.4 trillion in trading volume. Users can learn more at https://pancake.xyz/.
