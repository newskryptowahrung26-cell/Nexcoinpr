The Unique Maps Co. Marks Its 42nd Anniversary With the Launch of Its Unique 3D Map Collection

LONDON, UNITED KINGDOM, October 23rd, 2026

The London-designed map and art company combines historical cartography with modern elevation data to make mountains, valleys and coastlines appear to rise from the page.

﻿The Unique Maps Co., established in 1984, is marking its 42nd anniversary with the launch of its Unique 3D Map collection. The collection pairs historical cartography with modern elevation data, using rendered terrain and shadow to make landscapes appear to rise from a perfectly flat printed page.

The launch caps more than four decades of bringing remarkable maps and artwork into homes and commercial spaces, from restored historical maps and fine art reproductions to personalised maps celebrating favourite places, family stories and memorable journeys.

How the 3D Maps Work

Each piece in the collection starts with real elevation data. The company renders the terrain and casts shadows across it, then brings that depth together with the detail and character of traditional cartography. The result is a map where mountain ranges, valleys and coastlines seem to lift off the surface, even though the finished print itself remains flat.

The approach builds on the contemporary 3D shaded relief maps the company already creates, and it extends to personalisation. Customers can commission a personalised 3D relief map of almost any place in the world, turning a favourite mountain range, a home town or a stretch of coastline into a statement piece.

From Meaningful Gifts to Five-Star Interiors

Many of the company’s pieces are bought as gifts for weddings, anniversaries, birthdays, retirements and housewarmings, or as lasting reminders of somewhere special. Its range also includes specialist designs such as personalised golf course maps, ski resort and piste maps and satellite imagery.

Alongside its consumer business, The Unique Maps Co. works closely with hotels, offices, restaurants and interior designers to create distinctive interiors. According to the company, this now includes partnerships with luxury five-star hotels to provide artwork for bedrooms and communal spaces. Commercial clients can choose from bespoke sizes, curated collections and personalisation, from a single reception centrepiece to coordinated artwork across multiple rooms or locations, along with corporate and client gifts.

Growing From Strength to Strength

The company has grown into one of the most reviewed names in its category, with more than 8,000 5-star reviews and an Excellent rating on Trustpilot. Its personalised map gifts have become a major part of that growth, and the company expects to send out its one millionth personalised map gift in 2027.

What sets the business apart, it says, is the combination of museum-grade reproduction maps and artwork, exceptional large-format options, extensive choice and fast worldwide delivery. Pieces are designed in London and produced locally through an international network, which means many orders arrive within just a few days.

Forty-two years after it began, The Unique Maps Co. is still doing what it set out to do, bringing remarkable art and cartography within easy reach, now with landscapes that seem to rise from the page.

About The Unique Maps Co.

Established in 1984, The Unique Maps Co. brings the world’s most fascinating maps and artwork into homes and commercial spaces through museum-grade reproductions, expert restoration and personalisation. Its collections span restored historical maps, contemporary 3D shaded relief maps, fine art reproductions and vintage posters, available as unframed prints, framed prints and framed canvases. Designed in London and produced locally through an international network, its pieces ship worldwide. More information is available at uniquemaps.com.
