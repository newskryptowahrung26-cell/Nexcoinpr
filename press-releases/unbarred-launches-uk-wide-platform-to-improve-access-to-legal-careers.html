Unbarred Launches UK-Wide Platform to Improve Access to Legal Careers

BIRMINGHAM, UNITED KINGDOM, November 22nd, 2026

A promising new UK-wide platform aims to drive positive social impact by making legal careers more accessible through mentoring, career resources and professional opportunities, with a commitment to providing free support to aspiring lawyers from disadvantaged backgrounds.

A Birmingham-based trainee solicitor is drawing on her own experiences of adversity to help challenge perceptions of who can succeed in law and make the UK legal profession more accessible to aspiring lawyers.

Unbarred, a nationwide digital platform founded by Suman Rida, has already reached an audience of 16,000 within its first week of launch, with more than 250 people expressing interest in the initiative.

The encouraging early response reflects the interest surrounding a purpose-driven platform seeking to address unequal access to career guidance, professional networks and opportunities within the legal profession.

“Talent is everywhere. Opportunity should be too,” said Suman Rida, Founder of UNBARRED.

The encouraging early response reflects the interest surrounding a purpose-driven platform seeking to address one of the legal profession’s longstanding challenges: unequal access to career guidance, professional networks and opportunities.

Behind the initiative is a founder whose own journey into law has been shaped by many of the barriers she is now determined to help others overcome.

Raised by a single mother, Suman experienced significant financial hardship throughout her upbringing. During her teenage years, she also experienced homelessness, disrupting her education and leaving considerable gaps in her academic development.

When she eventually expressed her ambition to study A-levels and become a lawyer, she was met with discouragement rather than encouragement. One teacher suggested that she pursue a BTEC qualification and become a housewife, telling her that ‘people like her did not become lawyers’.

For Suman, those words became a source of determination.

Without the financial resources to access private tutoring or additional mentoring, she took responsibility for rebuilding her education. She spent hours reading books, researching online and developing the academic skills she had missed during her disrupted schooling.

Her determination eventually led her to pursue legal qualifications, build a career within the profession and secure a training contract.

Her journey illustrates how educational disadvantage and a lack of access to guidance can create barriers for capable and ambitious individuals, long before they reach the stage of applying for legal roles.

Rather than viewing her experiences solely as obstacles she had overcome, Suman began considering how she could use them to make the journey easier for others.

“I know what it feels like to have ambition but not the resources, guidance or connections to pursue it. There were times when people didn’t believe I belonged in the legal profession, but I refused to let my circumstances or other people’s expectations define my future. I had to find my own way, and I want to make that journey easier for others. Unbarred is my way of giving back to society and using the opportunities I’ve worked towards to help open doors for someone else” – said Suman.

Unbarred was created around a simple principle: an individual’s ability to pursue a career in law should not depend on their personal connections or socioeconomic background.

Opening Doors to the Legal Profession

From securing work experience and navigating training contract applications to understanding qualification routes and developing commercial awareness, the journey can involve challenges that are particularly difficult to overcome without reliable guidance.

Unbarred seeks to address these challenges through an accessible, digital-first approach, bringing practical career resources, professional insights and mentoring opportunities together on a single platform.

Central to the initiative is its commitment to ensuring that financial circumstances do not prevent aspiring lawyers from accessing the guidance they need. The platform provides free support to individuals from disadvantaged backgrounds, helping to remove some of the financial barriers associated with pursuing a legal career.

The platform offers resources covering CVs, applications, legal career pathways and commercial awareness, alongside articles and insights contributed by professionals working within the legal industry.

One of the most encouraging aspects of Unbarred’s launch has been the response from individuals already working within the legal profession.

Since its launch, the platform has attracted mentors from a range of professional backgrounds and levels of experience, including master’s students, trainee solicitors, qualified solicitors with more than five years of post-qualification experience and legal directors.

Their involvement demonstrates a willingness among legal professionals to share their knowledge, experience and guidance with the next generation.

The mentoring programme is designed to connect aspiring lawyers with individuals who can offer practical advice, professional insight and encouragement as they navigate their legal careers.

The diversity of experience among those who have signed up is particularly valuable, offering perspectives from individuals who have recently navigated legal education and recruitment, as well as those with established careers in the profession.

Beyond individual mentoring, Unbarred is also developing opportunities for collaboration with law firms, legal recruitment agencies and industry professionals, encouraging wider participation in initiatives that support aspiring lawyers from disadvantaged backgrounds.

These collaborations could provide opportunities for organisations to share recruitment expertise, contribute educational resources and engage with emerging legal talent, creating a model for greater industry collaboration and social impact.

While still in its early stages, Unbarred has made an encouraging start, attracting interest from aspiring lawyers and professionals willing to contribute their experience.

What makes the initiative particularly compelling is how Suman has drawn on her own experiences of educational and financial disadvantage to create something that could benefit others. Rather than allowing those challenges to define her career, she is now using her position within the profession to help widen access and encourage others to pursue their ambitions.

The platform’s longer-term ambition is to develop a nationwide community where aspiring lawyers can access professional guidance, build meaningful connections and pursue opportunities that might otherwise remain out of reach.

By combining lived experience, a digital-first approach, industry collaboration and a commitment to social mobility, Unbarred represents a promising contribution to efforts to make the legal profession more accessible.

Beyond widening access to legal careers, Unbarred also hopes to challenge the perception that the legal profession is exclusive or difficult to approach. While barriers to entry remain, the enthusiastic response from legal professionals willing to share their time, knowledge and experience highlights a supportive and welcoming side of the profession that is not always recognised. By bringing aspiring lawyers and established professionals together, Unbarred seeks to celebrate this willingness to help, encourage greater collaboration and ensure that financial circumstances do not prevent talented individuals from pursuing a career in law.

For Suman, the mission remains rooted in the belief that shaped her own journey: talent is everywhere, and opportunity should be too.

About Unbarred

Unbarred is a UK-wide digital platform founded by Suman Rida, a Birmingham-based trainee solicitor, to improve access to careers in the legal profession. The platform provides career guidance, mentoring opportunities, educational resources and professional insights for aspiring lawyers across the United Kingdom. With a focus on social mobility and equal opportunity, Unbarred offers free support to individuals from disadvantaged backgrounds. The initiative also encourages collaboration with legal professionals, law firms and recruitment organisations to help aspiring lawyers develop professional connections and navigate legal career pathways.
