Rome, Italy, October 10th, 2026

ANS Rome 2026 to Bring Together 300+ iGaming Professionals During SIGMA

WintEvents is preparing to host ANS Rome 2026, an exclusive networking event for the iGaming industry, taking place on November 4, 2026, in Rome, Italy, alongside SIGMA Europe.

The event is expected to welcome more than 300 iGaming professionals, bringing together industry representatives for an evening focused on meaningful networking and new business connections.

ANS Rome 2026 will combine professional networking with premium cuisine, drinks and entertainment, creating an informal environment for industry peers to meet, exchange ideas and strengthen relationships.

The event continues the Affiliate Networking Series concept of bringing the iGaming community together through carefully curated networking experiences.

About WintEvents

WintEvents is an international event organizer connecting professionals across the iGaming and affiliate marketing industries through business and networking events.

About ANS

Affiliate Networking Series (ANS) is WintEvents networking event series, bringing together affiliates, operators, agencies, and industry partners in key locations worldwide.
