KVB Accelerates Middle East Footprint with Strategic MENA Regional Expansion

Dubai, United Arab Emirates, October 6th, 2026 KVB , a global financial service platform provider, is expanding its presence in the...

Issuer: KVB • Source: Original Publication → • Published: 6 October 2026

Commercial Content Disclosure: The following announcement is an official press release syndicated on behalf of KVB (view source publication). NexcoinPR provides media distribution and editorial hosting. This content does not represent independent editorial reporting or investment advice.

Dubai, United Arab Emirates, October 6th, 2026

KVB, a global financial service platform provider, is expanding its presence in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region as part of its broader global development strategy. The company is strengthening its regional capabilities and establishing deeper connections across the market as it responds to developments in the region’s financial services market.

KVB marked a milestone in its regional development by participating in Forex Expo Dubai 2026 as a Featured Sponsor on September 22, 23 at Dubai World Trade Centre, the largest forex trading expo in the Middle East. The sponsorship featured a sports car display and branded conference seating, reflecting KVB’s focus on performance, technology and innovation, while also supporting its commitment to industry education and knowledge-sharing. As KVB’s first major industry event in the region, the expo provided an opportunity to engage with the wider forex community and exchange perspectives on the industry’s development.

Forex Expo Dubai 2026 Traders Hall

“KVB sees strong potential in MENA, supported by its dynamic financial markets, growing trading community and increasing demand for technology-driven financial services,” said Dr. Billy Prakash, CEO of KVB. “Our participation in Forex Expo Dubai was an important first step in deepening our understanding of the market and hearing directly from traders, partners and industry professionals. As we continue to develop our presence, we remain focused on bringing KVB’s international experience and technology-driven capabilities together with a stronger understanding of local market needs.”

KVB is also taking steps to strengthen its local capabilities, including the preparation of a regional office and expansion of its local team. KVB has introduced Arabic as a language across its online platform and website, broadening accessibility for Arabic-speaking clients and market participants throughout the MENA region.

Looking ahead, KVB plans to continue developing its regional operations, investing in its people and technology, and strengthening relationships with clients and industry partners. The company looks forward to building on the momentum of 2026 and further developing its presence in MENA in 2027 and beyond.

About KVB

Founded in 2001, KVB is a global financial service platform provider serving clients across more than 100 countries and regions. With more than 25 years of industry experience, KVB combines financial market expertise with technology to provide online trading solutions across forex, commodities, indices, shares and cryptocurrencies.

KVB continues to invest in proprietary financial technology, trading platforms, market analysis and risk management capabilities to support clients in an evolving global market. Through its international network and technology-driven approach, KVB is committed to providing accessible, reliable and innovative trading solutions for clients worldwide.
