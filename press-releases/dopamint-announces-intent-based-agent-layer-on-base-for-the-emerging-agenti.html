DopaMint Announces Intent-Based Agent Layer on Base for the Emerging Agentic Economy

San Francisco, USA, October 5th, 2026 DopaMint has announced the development of its House of Intent-Based Agents on Base, introducing an...

San Francisco, USA, October 5th, 2026

DopaMint has announced the development of its House of Intent-Based Agents on Base, introducing an intent-driven layer designed to help users turn natural-language requests into coordinated actions across applications, services, and on-chain infrastructure. Through its Dope orchestration layer, DopaMint connects users with more than 250 specialized agents designed to coordinate research, transactions, business tasks, financial activities, and everyday digital services.

As artificial intelligence agents increasingly move beyond providing information toward taking actions on behalf of users, DopaMint is building an infrastructure layer centered on a simple interaction model: users express what they want, while specialized agents coordinate the steps required to accomplish the request.

Building an Intent-Based Agent Economy

DopaMint’s House of Intent-Based Agents is designed to bring together specialized agents across areas including markets and money, business operations, professional services, personal life, and creative and technical work.

Rather than requiring users to navigate individual applications and manually determine each step of a task, the platform is designed to interpret user intent and coordinate the appropriate agents, tools, and execution processes.

A user could request assistance with tasks such as finding and booking a restaurant, planning a trip, researching a market, managing capital, handling a payment, completing a business operation, or interacting with onchain services. Agents can then coordinate the underlying workflow according to defined permissions and guardrails.

Dope Orchestration Layer

At the center of DopaMint’s platform is Dope, an orchestration layer designed to coordinate more than 250 specialized agents.

The system is intended to allow multiple agents to contribute to a single user request rather than requiring users to identify and operate each specialized service independently. Through an iMessage-style conversational experience, users can communicate their objectives while agents handle the coordination and execution required to complete the task.

This model positions intent as the starting point of the interaction, with agents responsible for determining how that intent can be translated into actionable steps.

Building on Base Infrastructure

DopaMint is developing its platform on Base, an Ethereum Layer 2 ecosystem that provides infrastructure for onchain applications and transactions.

The company is building around an emerging set of capabilities intended to support agentic applications, including agentic wallets, x402 payments, Base MCP, and onchain agent identity. These technologies can provide agents with mechanisms to interact with applications, access services, make payments, and participate in onchain activity.

DopaMint’s approach is to build an intent-driven layer on top of this infrastructure, connecting users and their requests with agents capable of coordinating activities across the broader digital and onchain ecosystem.

From Human Intent to Agent Execution

The company believes the development of agentic infrastructure could change how people interact with digital services. Instead of learning the interface and workflow of every application, users can communicate an objective in natural language and allow agents to determine the necessary steps.

This approach extends beyond cryptocurrency-focused activities. DopaMint is designed to support everyday digital and lifestyle tasks alongside financial, professional, technical, and onchain use cases.

As more applications and services become accessible to autonomous software agents, DopaMint is positioning its intent-based architecture as a way for users to interact with increasingly complex digital infrastructure through a simpler conversational experience.

“The next phase of crypto will be about more than people using applications. It will be about agents using those applications, paying for services and coordinating with other agents,” said Jawwy, Co-Founder of DopaMint. “DopaMint is being built around a simple idea: users should be able to state what they want, while agents handle the complexity underneath.”

Toward an Agentic Economy

DopaMint’s development reflects a broader shift toward software agents that can reason, coordinate, and transact rather than simply provide information.

The company is building its platform around the premise that users should be able to communicate their intent while specialized agents manage the underlying complexity. As agent-accessible infrastructure continues to expand, DopaMint aims to provide an interaction layer connecting human intent with coordinated agent execution.

About DopaMint

DopaMint is a House of Intent-Based Agents built on Base. Through Dope, its orchestration layer, DopaMint connects users with more than 250 specialized agents across markets and money, business operations, professional services, personal life, and creative and technical work.
