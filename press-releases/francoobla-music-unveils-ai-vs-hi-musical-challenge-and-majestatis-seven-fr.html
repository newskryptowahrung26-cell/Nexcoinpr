Francoobla Music Unveils AI vs. HI Musical Challenge and MAJESTATIS SEVEN Framework

Azzate, Italy, October 22nd, 2026 Italian musician Franco Oblatore invites music industry leaders and AI developers to explore...

Azzate, Italy, October 22nd, 2026

Italian musician Franco Oblatore invites music industry leaders and AI developers to explore human-directed music creation, a proposed similarity assessment framework, and an upcoming international music project.

Francoobla Music, the independent musical project founded by Italian musician and composer Franco Oblatore, has announced a two-part international initiative focused on the relationship between human creativity, artificial intelligence, and intellectual property.

The initiative introduces the International Friendly Musical Challenge Between AI and HI (Human Intelligence) and MAJESTATIS SEVEN, a proposed framework for identifying defined similarities in musical compositions and written lyrics.

Alongside these initiatives, Francoobla Music is preparing an international music project featuring Lady Jane, a virtual singer persona associated with a catalog of approximately 200 tracks developed through human-directed and AI-assisted creative processes, according to the project’s founder.

Oblatore, who describes his musical experience as spanning 60 years, is inviting music professionals, broadcasting organizations, technology developers, and industry representatives to examine the projects and explore opportunities for international collaboration.

Part I: International Friendly Musical Challenge Between AI and HI

The International Friendly Musical Challenge Between AI and HI is a proposed creative competition designed to explore the relationship between human artistic direction and generative artificial intelligence.

The initiative aims to demonstrate how musicians can use AI-assisted production tools while maintaining creative control over musical arrangements, instrumentation, rhythm, composition, and vocal interpretation.

According to Oblatore, his approach involves detailed musical instructions developed through his experience as a musician and composer.

These instructions cover musical elements such as tempo, instrumental arrangements, melodic direction, rhythmic structures, solos, and vocal performances.

The process incorporates references to instruments including the Hammond C3 organ, Steinway piano, Gibson guitars, and Fender Stratocaster guitars, alongside the development of vocal performances for the project’s virtual artist, Lady Jane.

Rather than positioning artificial intelligence as a replacement for musicians, Francoobla Music presents the challenge as an opportunity to examine how human musical knowledge can guide the use of generative technology.

“I am an ordinary person with 60 years of concrete experience, registered patents, and a human-directed AI catalog built since 2023. I invite the industry to look closely at what we have developed to assess the significant improvements from 2023 to 2026,” Oblatore states.

Francoobla Music is inviting U.S. financial media, broadcasting networks, record labels, AI developers, and other interested organizations to evaluate the initiative.

The project may also pursue opportunities in Asian markets, including China, India, South Korea, and Japan.

“We are offering a clear window for review and discussion,” says Oblatore. “Should new international avenues align better with our long-term goals, Francoobla Music could consider directing its strategic initiatives toward Asian markets.”

Details concerning participation requirements, evaluation criteria, and the timing of the proposed musical challenge have not yet been announced.

Part II: MAJESTATIS SEVEN , A Proposed Framework for Assessing Creative Similarities

The second component of the initiative is MAJESTATIS SEVEN, a proposed methodology developed by Francoobla Music to identify defined sequences of matching elements in music and written content.

The framework introduces two criteria intended to provide structured reference points for examining similarities between creative works.

Under the proposed methodology, musical or written sequences meeting these criteria would be identified for further examination.

Francoobla Music presents the framework as a potential contribution to discussions involving originality, authorship, copyright protection, and the use of generative AI in creative industries.

The proposed criteria are intended to provide a defined basis for similarity assessment, particularly in situations where musicians, publishers, and technology developers are examining content produced through traditional or AI-assisted methods.

MAJESTATIS SEVEN is a proposed assessment methodology, not an established legal standard for determining plagiarism or copyright infringement. Any legal determination would remain subject to applicable copyright laws and the circumstances of the individual work.

Francoobla Music welcomes discussion with music industry representatives, intellectual property professionals, and AI developers interested in evaluating the proposed approach.

Lady Jane and the Upcoming International Musical Kolossal

As part of its broader international initiative, Francoobla Music is preparing the International Musical Kolossal, a music project intended to showcase compositions developed through human artistic direction and AI-assisted production.

Central to the project is Lady Jane, a virtual singer persona featured in the Francoobla Music catalog.

According to Oblatore, the catalog includes approximately 200 original tracks spanning multiple musical genres.

The planned initial presentation will feature 24 selected tracks, including four Italian pop compositions, intended to demonstrate the project’s musical range and creative approach.

Francoobla Music identifies the United States, the European Union, China, and Japan among the markets being considered for the project’s international introduction.

The planned release will focus on the relationship between human creative direction and emerging music production technologies, highlighting the role of musical experience in guiding AI-assisted compositions.

Further announcements regarding the project’s release schedule, distribution arrangements, and international presentation are expected as preparations progress.

These references are provided by Francoobla Music for informational purposes and remain subject to independent verification.

The existence of a registration reference or case number does not independently establish that a proposed methodology has been adopted as a legally binding international copyright standard.

International Engagement and Next Steps

Through the combined initiatives, Francoobla Music aims to encourage dialogue among musicians, composers, recording industry professionals, media organizations, technology developers, and intellectual property specialists.

The International Friendly Musical Challenge Between AI and HI is intended to highlight the role of human creative direction in AI-assisted music production, while MAJESTATIS SEVEN proposes a structured approach for examining selected similarities in music and written content.

The forthcoming International Musical Kolossal will provide a creative component to these discussions through Lady Jane and the project’s selected musical works.

Francoobla Music is inviting interested industry participants to review the initiatives and contact the project directly to discuss potential collaboration, technical evaluation, or international presentation opportunities.

About Francoobla Music

Francoobla Music is an independent musical project founded by Franco Oblatore, an Italian musician, composer, and former industrialist based in Azzate, Italy.

Drawing on Oblatore’s six decades of musical experience, the project explores the relationship between human artistic direction and emerging technologies, including generative artificial intelligence.

Its initiatives include original musical compositions, the virtual artist persona Lady Jane, the International Friendly Musical Challenge Between AI and HI, and the proposed MAJESTATIS SEVEN framework.

Through these projects, Francoobla Music seeks to encourage international discussion about creativity, musical authorship, and intellectual property in the evolving music and technology industries.
