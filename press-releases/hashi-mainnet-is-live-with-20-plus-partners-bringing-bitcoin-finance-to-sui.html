Hashi Mainnet is Live With 20-Plus Partners, Bringing Bitcoin Finance to Sui at Scale

Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands, November 19th, 2026 Anchorage Digital, Fluid, HoneyB, and Upshift support institutional and retail access...

Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands, November 19th, 2026

Anchorage Digital, Fluid, HoneyB, and Upshift support institutional and retail access to Bitcoin on Sui with BitGo, Inc. expected to follow in 2027

Hashi, Sui's native Bitcoin finance infrastructure, is now live on mainnet, with more than 20 custody, retail, and DeFi participants.

Bitcoin has spent years becoming one of the world's largest pools of capital. Now the infrastructure to put it to work has arrived, forming one of the broadest coalitions in Bitcoin finance. 

That coalition is still growing fast. The latest to join Hashi are Merkl, an onchain incentive platform; and Current, a lending protocol. These additions expand the ecosystem of DeFi products and services being built around Bitcoin on Sui.

Bitcoin-Native Finance, Now Live

Hashi keeps Bitcoin on the Bitcoin network and mints hBTC, a 1:1 Bitcoin-backed asset, on Sui. That hBTC can then serve as collateral for lending, borrowing, and other onchain financial products. 

Hashi is decentralized by design. It's secured by validators, not a single entity, using industry-leading cryptography. On top of that foundation, it connects leading providers across custody, trading, and decentralized finance to build financial products, giving Bitcoin holders many ways to put their assets to work.

Accessing Bitcoin Finance on Sui

With hBTC now live, Bitcoin holders have a new set of opportunities at their fingertips. That starts with borrowing against BTC and accessing new financial strategies through lending markets and vaults. 

To meet the specific needs of institutions, several new providers are offering new lending and vault facilities. 

Fluid’s institutional-grade lending protocol is now live on Sui, beginning with Bitcoin-backed stablecoin loans. Bitcoin holders can use Hashi to bring BTC onto Sui as hBTC, then use it as collateral on Fluid to borrow stablecoins, starting with USDC with more flows coming. 

“Bitcoin is the largest pool of idle capital in crypto,” said Samyak Jain, founder and CEO at Fluid. “With Hashi, it can now power deep, capital-efficient credit markets on Fluid.”

Institutional Access, Retail Opportunities, and Liquidity

For institutions, that infrastructure shows up as a custody problem solved. Institutions will be able to use both leading crypto custodians, Anchorage Digital and BitGo, Inc. (“BitGo”) to access Hashi. This model gives institutions a path to hold or deploy BTC into Hashi. Previously announced institutional partners, such as Cumberland and Wave Digital Assets will all play roles as early participants in deploying BTC and stablecoins on Hashi, providing liquidity to a variety of strategies on Sui. Sui DeFi protocols, including Blueprint Finance (Concrete), Bluefin, Cetus, Current, and Suilend will also unlock new lending opportunities, stablecoin credit, and yield strategies backed by Bitcoin. 

“Some of the world's largest Bitcoin holders are sitting on enormous amounts of untapped financial potential,” said Nathan McCauley, CEO and Co-Founder at Anchorage Digital. “Hashi gives our institutional clients a new way to access Bitcoin finance on Sui, with their BTC held in custody at Anchorage Digital Bank.”

If you’re an institution interested in deploying BTC or stablecoins on Hashi, please reach out to your custodian or hashi@sui.io. Users can explore more at sui.io/hashi. 

About Sui

Sui, where money moves as freely as messages, is a next-generation Layer 1 blockchain built for agentic finance and scalable global payments. Founded by the core team behind Meta’s stablecoin initiative and powered by an object-centric model, Sui makes assets, permissions, and user data programmable and ownable. Sui’s primitives offer builders everything they need to create high-performance payments and financial applications, including instant agentic payments. Learn more at sui.io.
