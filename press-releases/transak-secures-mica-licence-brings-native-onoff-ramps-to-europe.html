Transak Secures MiCA Licence, Brings Native On/Off-Ramps to Europe

Miami, Florida, November 25th, 2026 Used by more than 600 apps and 10 million users, Transak now adds Europe to a regulated footprint...

Issuer: Transak • Source: Decrypt → • Published: 25 November 2026

Commercial Content Disclosure: The following announcement is an official press release syndicated from Decrypt on behalf of Transak. NexcoinPR provides media distribution and editorial hosting. This content does not represent independent editorial reporting or investment advice.

Miami, Florida, November 25th, 2026

Used by more than 600 apps and 10 million users, Transak now adds Europe to a regulated footprint spanning the US, the UK, Canada and Australia, and is bringing native on- and off-ramps to European users.

Transak, the payments infrastructure company connecting traditional money with stablecoins and crypto, today announced that its Vienna-based entity, Transak GmbH, has been authorised by Austria's Financial Market Authority (FMA) under the EU's Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA). From 26 November, the apps that integrate Transak can offer regulated on- and off-ramps to users across all 30 countries of the European Economic Area through a single integration.

Stablecoins are fast becoming how money moves online, and a new generation of neobanks, wallets and fintechs is being built on them. Every one of those apps needs a regulated bridge to local money in each market it serves. With MiCA, Transak now provides that bridge across the world's largest markets.

One licence for Europe

The authorisation lets users buy stablecoins and other crypto-assets with euros and other fiat currencies, and sell them back to their bank accounts. This extends across the European Economic Area, so partners can reach users from Lisbon to Helsinki through one integration, with Transak GmbH as the authorised provider behind every transaction.

Transak built its European business from the ground up in Vienna, with its own entity, local leadership and direct connections to European banking rails. Services go live on 26 November with cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and SEPA, and some of the world's largest wallets will go live with Transak for their European users in the coming weeks.

“MiCA has put clarity on what stablecoins are and are not allowed to do in Europe, and that lets us roll out our services across the region with confidence,” said Sami Start, Co-founder and CEO of Transak. “With increasing clarity comes increasing confidence for companies to invest in large projects that use stablecoins to power their backend. We’re really excited to keep building products that our partners can use to serve their users across the European Economic Area, as well as in the US, the UK, Canada, Australia and the other markets we’re live in.”

One integration for global apps

MiCA joins Transak's licences and registrations across the US, the UK, Canada and Australia, making it one of the most extensively regulated on- and off-ramp providers in the world. Each market runs on Transak's own entities, banking rails and compliance, and the footprint keeps growing as Transak expands into new markets.

For a fintech building globally, that footprint is the point. Integrate Transak once, and every new licence becomes a new market where users can move between their local money and stablecoins, whether that's a neobank offering digital dollar accounts, a wallet, a card top-up or a trading app. Partners own the customer and the experience. Transak owns everything behind it, which is why more than 600 apps and over 10 million users already rely on it.

Native by default

Getting into crypto has always been harder than it should be. Users opened exchange accounts, repeated identity checks, navigated peer-to-peer markets or got bounced to a third-party widget mid-flow, and every step lost people. The apps now being built on stablecoins can't afford that. The ramp has to disappear into the product.

That is why Transak built Native Ramps: APIs that let apps run on- and off-ramps entirely inside their own interface. MetaMask went live with Native Ramps last year, giving its users an in-app deposit experience they never have to leave, and a growing number of partners have since followed. With MiCA in place, European partners can now ship those native flows to their users, backed by an EU-authorised provider.

About Transak

Transak is the payments infrastructure that connects traditional money with stablecoins and crypto. Through a single API, wallets, neobanks, fintechs and marketplaces let users onboard, fund and withdraw directly inside their products. More than 600 apps have used Transak, serving over 10 million users worldwide. Learn more at transak.com.
