Fintech Media Group Expands European Market Data Access Through FINTECH.TV Terminal

NEW YORK, NY, November 19th, 2026 European market data, localized financial news and 24/7 global broadcasting are now available through...

NexcoinPR provides media distribution and editorial hosting. This content does not represent independent editorial reporting or investment advice.

NEW YORK, NY, November 19th, 2026

European market data, localized financial news and 24/7 global broadcasting are now available through FINTECH.TV’s customizable European Terminal experience.

Fintech Media Group (FTMG), a global financial media and technology company with daily broadcasts from the New York Stock Exchange and Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange, studios at City Walk in Dubai, and live coverage from the Cboe trading floor in Chicago, today announced that its European FINTECH.TV Terminal experience is now live. The platform brings together regional market data, localized financial news and 24/7 global financial broadcasting within a customizable digital environment, providing European audiences with a dedicated regional market experience alongside international financial coverage and market intelligence.

With regional terminal environments already established across the Middle East, Canada, India and Latin America, the company’s expansion into Europe represents another milestone in its strategy to connect investors and financial professionals with information relevant to their regional markets while maintaining access to global financial intelligence.

Unlike traditional financial terminals designed primarily for institutional users, the FINTECH.TV Terminal combines live financial broadcasting, market data, localized news, customizable widgets and watchlists within a unified digital experience. Built for retail investors, professional traders, financial advisors, family offices and other market participants, the platform connects regional market intelligence with global financial coverage while supporting Fintech Media Group’s broader investment technology strategy.

“Europe represents a natural next step in the international expansion of the FINTECH.TV Terminal,” said Vince Molinari, CEO of Fintech Media Group. “European capital markets are among the most established and influential in the world, with investors following a diverse range of exchanges, currencies, industries and economic developments. The FINTECH.TV Terminal now combines localized European market intelligence with live financial programming from the world’s leading exchanges, giving audiences a more connected view of opportunities across regional and international markets. We believe the future of financial media is about connecting information, research and investment technology in ways that make global markets more accessible and actionable.”

Connecting Europe’s Major Financial Markets Through a Unified Terminal

Fintech Media Group’s European expansion introduces market data from major European indexes, publicly traded companies and foreign exchange markets into the FINTECH.TV Terminal, strengthening the platform’s international financial intelligence capabilities.

The integration connects European market activity with U.S. equities, international currencies and global financial developments, allowing investors and financial professionals to monitor regional markets alongside broader international trends.

Localized Market Intelligence Across European Economies

The European market data integration features exchange-traded instruments tracking two of the region’s most widely followed equity benchmarks: Germany’s DAX and the EURO STOXX 50, an index representing major blue-chip companies across the eurozone. The FINTECH.TV Terminal displays pricing, daily performance changes and visual price trends for these instruments, providing investors with visibility into European equity market activity.

The Terminal also features market data for major European companies, including SAP, a leading German enterprise software company; ASML, a Netherlands-based semiconductor equipment manufacturer; and LVMH, a French luxury goods group. Users can view displayed market prices, daily price changes expressed in absolute and percentage terms, and visual price trends, providing a snapshot of companies representing key sectors of the European economy, including enterprise technology, semiconductor manufacturing and luxury goods.

Foreign exchange coverage includes the EUR/USD currency pair, while commodity market data includes Brent crude oil. Together, these instruments provide additional context around currency movements, energy prices, international trade and broader market developments influencing European economies.

The European Terminal experience also incorporates localized financial news and market headlines through customizable content modules. Regional news coverage appears alongside market data and international financial developments, helping users follow stories relevant to European economies while maintaining visibility into global markets.

The shared technology infrastructure supports localized market intelligence within Fintech Media Group’s broader international terminal platform, delivering a consistent financial media experience while highlighting the companies, indexes and currencies relevant to European investors.

Connecting European Audiences With FINTECH.TV’s Global Broadcast Network

A central component of the European expansion is FINTECH.TV’s established international financial media network.

FINTECH.TV broadcasts daily from the New York Stock Exchange and Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange, with studio operations in Dubai and live coverage from Cboe in Chicago.

The company’s programming connects audiences with market developments, financial executives, investors, policymakers and technology leaders shaping the global financial system.

Through the FINTECH.TV Terminal, European users can access FINTECH.TV’s 24/7 live financial broadcasting, on-demand programming and additional financial news channels alongside regional market data and localized news. The integrated viewing experience allows audiences to follow international financial coverage while monitoring market developments within the same customizable platform.

The integration provides additional context around developments affecting European investors, including U.S. monetary policy, international capital flows, global trade, energy markets, emerging technologies and the continued evolution of digital assets.

“Europe represents an important opportunity to expand both our reach and relevance,” said Kevin Connor, Chief Marketing Officer of FINTECH.TV. “With our European Terminal experience now live, we are connecting European audiences with locally relevant market intelligence while introducing companies, executives and financial institutions to FINTECH.TV’s broader global network. Combining our international broadcast distribution with a customizable digital experience strengthens how audiences discover, engage with and return to our content across markets.”

Connecting Financial Media With Investment Technology

The European expansion also builds on Fintech Media Group’s integrated financial technology infrastructure, including the TAP platform acquired through an all-stock transaction valued at approximately $50 million.

TAP’s technology combines artificial intelligence, market intelligence, digital wallets, brokerage connectivity, investment strategies and blockchain-based financial infrastructure, supporting Fintech Media Group’s broader broadcast-to-brokerage vision.

The platform includes more than 50 thematic, sector-based, multi-asset and target-date investment strategies designed to address a range of investment objectives and market opportunities.

Over time, Fintech Media Group intends to explore the integration of relevant investment strategies and financial services into European terminal environments, subject to applicable regulatory requirements, licensing arrangements and supported market access.

Potential applications include European equity strategies, international investment baskets and strategies focused on energy, infrastructure, technology, precious metals, digital assets and other investment themes. 

The company’s longer-term objective is to connect financial broadcasting and market research with eligible investment opportunities through supported financial service providers where permitted by applicable laws and regulations.

The European terminal expansion provides localized financial media and market intelligence capabilities and does not, by itself, establish the availability of brokerage or investment services in any particular European jurisdiction.

Expanding a Global Financial Media and Technology Platform

With European market data now live, Fintech Media Group’s international terminal platform spans markets across North America, Europe, the Middle East, India and Latin America.

Through the FINTECH.TV Terminal, the company provides a more accessible and customizable alternative to traditional institutional financial terminals, which can cost thousands of dollars per seat annually.

Its existing media distribution network, extending across approximately 2,500 hotel properties, 2,400 airport gates and 750 airport lounges, provides additional opportunities to introduce international audiences to FINTECH.TV and its integrated financial technology platform.

The global terminal architecture supports multiple revenue opportunities, including subscriptions, enterprise and white-label licensing, advertising, sponsorships, premium market intelligence and data partnerships. The European Terminal experience incorporates sponsored content and display advertising placements alongside financial programming and market information, enabling brands and financial institutions to engage audiences within a market-focused digital environment.

By operating localized terminal environments on a shared technology foundation, Fintech Media Group is expanding its international presence while maintaining a consistent approach to financial broadcasting, market intelligence and digital engagement.

The company’s broader strategy is to connect regional financial information with global markets, helping audiences follow developments locally, research opportunities internationally and access eligible investment technology where supported.

About Fintech Media Group

Fintech Media Group is a global financial media and technology company focused on connecting financial information, markets and investment opportunities through digital media, artificial intelligence and integrated financial technology.

Through FINTECH.TV and its TAP technology platform, the company combines 24/7 financial broadcasting, market intelligence, multi-asset investment strategies, brokerage connectivity and emerging digital asset infrastructure.

The company’s international presence includes financial media operations and relationships across major global financial centers, with localized financial terminal environments serving markets across North America, Europe, the Middle East, India and Latin America.

Fintech Media Group’s mission is to make global financial markets more accessible, intelligent and actionable for investors, financial institutions and strategic partners worldwide.

Learn more at FINTECH.TV.
