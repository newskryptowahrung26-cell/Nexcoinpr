LBank Ranks No. 1 for Mainstream Crypto ($52.16M) and Stock Depth (4.4x) in BeInCrypto Report

A full-scale market-depth study published by BeInCrypto has evaluated LBank’s order-book liquidity against six major exchanges, Binance...

Issuer: LBank • Source: Decrypt → • Published: 23 September 2026

Commercial Content Disclosure: The following announcement is an official press release syndicated from Decrypt on behalf of LBank. NexcoinPR provides media distribution and editorial hosting. This content does not represent independent editorial reporting or investment advice.

Singapore, Singapore, September 23rd, 2026

A full-scale market-depth study published by BeInCrypto has evaluated LBank’s order-book liquidity against six major exchanges, Binance, OKX, Gate, Bitget, MEXC, and Bybit. The report reveals that LBank secured the No. 1 ranking in five out of six major USDT spot trading pairs, including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), XRP, and BNB, while ranking second for Dogecoin (DOGE). Across all six sampled pairs, LBank’s displayed depth consistently exceeded the six-peer median.

The findings are particularly relevant to how liquidity is assessed beyond headline trading volume. In the study’s August 29 snapshots, LBank showed $52.16 million of displayed depth for BTC/USDT and $25.24 million for ETH/USDT, compared with $11.79 million and $8.15 million respectively on the strongest competing books in those comparisons. BeInCrypto notes that displayed order-book depth does not guarantee execution quality, but the results provide a measurable indication of the liquidity available around market prices in the sampled major pairs.

The research also points to a broader asset-access strategy. BeInCrypto examined selected emerging tokens alongside major markets and found that LBank had scheduled PONS trading 54 days and seven hours ahead of Bitget in the specific comparison. While the study does not treat individual token performance as evidence of typical customer returns, the listing comparison illustrates LBank’s focus on bringing emerging assets to market at an early stage. The report also examined selected assets that recorded substantial price movements after listing, while explicitly noting that the sample was not representative of LBank’s entire asset universe.

That combination of established liquidity and access to newer assets is also reflected in LBank’s broader market offering. The exchange currently lists more than 300 mainstream coins and over 50 high-potential gems, alongside spot and futures markets and its 100x Gems discovery tool. LBank has also ranked No. 1 in the 100x Gems, Highest Gains and Meme Share categories in its internal market positioning framework. These offerings reflect an approach that spans established assets and faster-moving segments of the crypto market, rather than concentrating liquidity and product development around a single asset class.

A further area highlighted by the BeInCrypto research is tokenized equities, where LBank’s market depth stood out across several products. The study screened 22 selected tokenized products spanning bStocks, Ondo, and xStocks, and found LBank leading nine of the 22 exact-product depth comparisons. In the tokenized equities segment (bStocks), LBank demonstrated pronounced order-book depth across Robinhood-linked product pairs. Specifically, LBank recorded $1.10 million in displayed depth for HOODON (compared to $248,125 on its top rival) and $564,324 for HOODX ($252,804 on the rival), representing 4.42x and 2.23x greater liquidity, respectively.

The Robinhood comparison is notable as tokenized equities move closer to the center of the broader crypto market structure. Robinhood launched its public blockchain in July with 24/7 Stock Token trading and positioned tokenized assets as a core part of its broader effort to connect traditional finance with onchain markets. The Block and CoinDesk have since tracked the rapid expansion of Robinhood Chain and the growing activity around tokenized stocks and other real-world assets. Against that backdrop, BeInCrypto’s findings place LBank’s Robinhood-linked markets within a broader trend toward bringing recognizable traditional financial exposures into crypto-native trading environments.

Security remains another component of the infrastructure behind this expansion. BeInCrypto’s review documented LBank’s ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification and its relationships with Elliptic and CertiK, while distinguishing between documented security arrangements and independently measured control outcomes. LBank has also maintained a 10-year operating track record with zero security incidents and holds ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification, while participating in the CertiK Crypto Resilience Initiative. Together, these measures form part of LBank’s broader approach to security, compliance and risk management as its product scope expands.

"As traditional finance and digital assets converge, traders demand institutional-grade order-book depth alongside access to emerging products," said Eric He, Community Angel Officer and Risk Control Advisor at LBank. "The findings from BeInCrypto validate our ongoing investments in core market liquidity, asset discovery, and rigorous risk control as we build a seamlessly connected trading environment for global users."

Looking ahead, LBank plans to continue expanding market depth across core crypto assets while broadening access to emerging tokens and tokenized financial products. The exchange expects continued development across liquidity, asset coverage, product infrastructure and security to support a more connected trading environment as the boundaries between traditional finance and digital assets continue to narrow.

The full BeInCrypto Market Lens study, including its methodology, market-depth comparisons and research limitations, is available through BeInCrypto.
