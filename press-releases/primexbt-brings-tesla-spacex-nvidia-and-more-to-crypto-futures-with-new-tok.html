PrimeXBT Brings Tesla, SpaceX, NVIDIA and More to Crypto Futures with New Tokenised Stocks

Issuer: PrimeXBT • Source: Decrypt → • Published: 27 October 2026

Commercial Content Disclosure: The following announcement is an official press release syndicated from Decrypt on behalf of PrimeXBT. NexcoinPR provides media distribution and editorial hosting. This content does not represent independent editorial reporting or investment advice.

Castries, Saint Lucia, October 27th, 2026

PrimeXBT, a global multi-asset broker and crypto asset service provider, has announced a major expansion of its offering, introducing Tokenised Stocks, a new category of Crypto Futures available on its native PXTrader 2.0 platform. The launch gives crypto traders access to price movements linked to some of the world's most prominent companies, bringing opportunities from traditional equity markets into the crypto trading environment.

The new category includes seven USDT-denominated Crypto Futures linked to SpaceX (SPCX/USDT), NVIDIA (NVDA/USDT), Tesla (TSLA/USDT), Circle (CRCL/USDT), Apple (AAPL/USDT), Alphabet (GOOGL/USDT) and Robinhood (HOOD/USDT). Traders can take long or short positions and respond to market developments around the clock, including outside traditional stock exchange hours. The contracts provide exposure to company-linked price movements without ownership of the underlying shares.

“Big Tech remains at the centre of market attention, with AI investment, corporate earnings and growth expectations driving sentiment across global equities. Earnings season in particular can bring significant price movements, as investors reassess company performance and future prospects,” commented Jonatan Randin, Senior Market Analyst at PrimeXBT. “With tokenised stocks, we're opening up new opportunities to crypto traders, allowing them to participate in major equity market trends through a familiar crypto trading environment.”

Alongside Tokenised Stocks, PrimeXBT has added Lighter (LIT/USDT), Monad (MON/USDT), Venice Token (VVV/USDT), Canton (CC/USDT), Stable (STABLE/USDT), JUST (JST/USDT) and Midnight (NIGHT/USDT) to its PXTrader 2.0 lineup of over 170 Crypto Futures, further strengthening the depth and diversity of its offering across DeFi, AI, Gaming, Payments, Layer 1 and 2 networks, blockchain infrastructure, NFTs, Meme coins and the Metaverse. The broker has also introduced new Crypto CFDs on Monero (XMR/USD), Hyperliquid (HYPE/USD) and Zcash (ZEC/USD) on PXTrader 2.0, with corresponding USDT-quoted instruments available on MetaTrader 5.

PrimeXBT has also expanded its blockchain network support with the addition of Arbitrum, Optimism and Base, joining existing networks including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tron, BNB Smart Chain and Solana. Additionally, the broker has added Tether Gold (XAUT), a tokenized gold asset, to its internal Exchange, expanding its offering of over 50 cryptocurrencies and digital assets.

The expanded offering is supported by flexible crypto funding and crypto-denominated accounts in USDT, USDC, BTC and ETH, allowing clients to trade without converting their holdings into fiat. PrimeXBT offers competitive Crypto Futures fees of 0.01% for makers and 0.045% for takers, with further discounts through its volume-based VIP program. New clients automatically receive VIP 4 status for their first 14 days, reducing Crypto Futures taker fees by 33%, while high-volume traders can unlock fees as low as 0.015% and Bitcoin CFD spreads from $19 at the highest VIP tier.

The launch reflects the growing convergence of crypto and traditional financial markets, bringing a wider range of global market trends into the crypto trading environment. From artificial intelligence and electric vehicles to space technology and digital finance, PrimeXBT continues to broaden trading opportunities beyond digital assets, reinforcing its position as an innovative multi-asset broker connecting markets through one trading ecosystem.

To learn more about the Crypto Futures offering, users can visit PrimeXBT’s website.

About PrimeXBT

PrimeXBT is a global multi-asset broker and crypto asset service provider trusted by traders in more than 150 countries. The platform bridges traditional and digital markets within one integrated environment, redefining versatility and innovation in online trading. Clients can access Forex, CFDs on indices, commodities, shares, crypto, and Crypto Futures, as well as buy, store and exchange cryptocurrencies. This unified experience extends across both the native PXTrader 2.0 platform and MetaTrader 5, supported by advanced risk-management tools and a wide range of funding options in crypto, fiat and local payment methods. Since 2018, PrimeXBT has focused on empowering traders through broad multi-asset access, fair and transparent conditions, professional-grade technology and dedicated human support. By combining expertise, trust and a client-first approach, PrimeXBT sets a benchmark of excellence in the financial industry and provides traders with the tools they need to trade, grow and succeed with confidence.
