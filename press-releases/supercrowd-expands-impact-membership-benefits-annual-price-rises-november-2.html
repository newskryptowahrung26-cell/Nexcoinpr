SuperCrowd Expands Impact Membership Benefits; Annual Price Rises November 27

STOCKTON, Calif., November 16th, 2026 The Super Crowd, Inc. , a public benefit corporation, today announced expanded Impact Membership...

Issuer: SuperCrowd • Source: Original Publication → • Published: 16 November 2026

Commercial Content Disclosure: The following announcement is an official press release syndicated on behalf of SuperCrowd (view source publication). NexcoinPR provides media distribution and editorial hosting. This content does not represent independent editorial reporting or investment advice.

STOCKTON, Calif., November 16th, 2026

The Super Crowd, Inc., a public benefit corporation, today announced expanded Impact Membership benefits and an annual subscription price increase from $55 to $75, effective Friday, November 27, for new subscribers.

“Most of today’s member benefits didn’t exist a year ago,” said Devin D. Thorpe, founder and CEO. “We’ve built a growing collection of research tools, connections and practical support to help investors and entrepreneurs make money a force for good.”

Membership includes the searchable SuperCrowd Investment Directory, unlimited AI due diligence reports from SuperImpactScore.ai, Devin’s Impact Pick of the Week, private networking and monthly live sessions. Annual members also receive a private 30-minute coaching session each year after their free trial.

Members save $100 on Campaign Diagnostics and select Raise Plans, $50 on every SuperCrowd Impact Wire package and $50 on Startup Showcase videos for SuperCrowd.tv.

Further enhancements are planned before year-end, including implementation of the Crowdfunding Professional Association’s Private Market Reporting Standard (PMRS) in the SuperCrowd Impact League portfolio tracker.

“Our goal is to keep delivering more value than membership costs,” Thorpe added. “At the new annual price, that’s just $6.25 a month for tools, relationships and support that can help members pursue their financial and impact goals.”

Impact Membership begins with a 30-day free trial. Anyone choosing an annual subscription before November 27 secures the $55 annual rate when billing begins and on future renewals. Current subscribers retain their existing rates.

Monthly Impact Membership is now $15.50. Max-Impact Membership remains $155 annually and does not include a free trial.

The SuperCrowd connects investors and purpose-driven entrepreneurs through Superpowers for Good, educational programming, research resources and services designed to expand participation in impact crowdfunding and social entrepreneurship.

Start a free trial and secure the current annual rate at s4g.biz/subscribe before November 27.

About the SuperCrowd

The Super Crowd, Inc., a public benefit corporation, helps everyday investors and purpose-driven entrepreneurs make money a force for good. Through Superpowers for Good, SuperCrowd.tv, investment research tools, educational programs, events and networking, the company equips people to build and back businesses that make a difference. Its work expands participation in impact crowdfunding, connects entrepreneurs with capital and helps investors align their financial goals with their values. Learn more at thesupercrowd.com.
