ChangeNOW Unveils TOKEN2049 Singapore Lineup, Bit Digital Stage Address, and Fast Track Wallet Program

Issuer: ChangeNOW • Source: Decrypt → • Published: 25 September 2026

Commercial Content Disclosure: The following announcement is an official press release syndicated from Decrypt on behalf of ChangeNOW. NexcoinPR provides media distribution and editorial hosting. This content does not represent independent editorial reporting or investment advice.

Kingstown, St. Vincent & The Grenadines, September 25th, 2026

Pauline Shangett brings the crypto super app argument to the Bit Digital Stage on October 7, and the booth opens a Fast Track Program zone for early-stage wallets.

ChangeNOW, an enterprise-focused crypto super app, has announced its program for TOKEN2049 Singapore. The company works the week on three fronts: a booth built around partner projects, a keynote on where crypto products go next, and an evening event of its own.

The ChangeNOW booth: Retrofuturism and Japanese Metabolism

The booth design combines retrofuturism with Japanese Metabolism, the 1960s architectural movement. Retrofuturism is the way the 60s pictured the future. It suits both Singapore and the pace of Web3. It also keeps the past in view: finance is centuries old, and crypto is its current chapter.

In architecture, Metabolism means separate capsules that connect into one building. ChangeNOW works the same way: many products, one app, many sources , one solution.

The team laid out one large open space split into working zones, open both days of the conference.

Podcast Studio

A podcast studio is at the center of the booth. For two days the ChangeNOW team will record interviews and other formats there with founders and product leads from across the industry. Major crypto media will use the same space for their own interviews during the conference.

Fast Track Program Zone

One zone is given to the Fast Track Program, a free ChangeNOW program for new and early-stage crypto wallets. A team gets an API key, adds swaps to the app, and starts earning a share of the volume its users generate. What a participant gets:

  • Swap infrastructure: fixed or floating rates, and revenue attribution from day one
  • Ecosystem support: integration help and access to the ChangeNOW partner network
  • PR and marketing: placements in crypto media with an estimated reach of 10M+, posts on ChangeNOW channels with a combined audience over 100,000, and a spot at tier-1 conferences that draw around 15K attendees

Visitors can see at the booth how the program works and how to apply.

ChangeNOW × CoolWallet zone

A limited run of co-branded CoolWallet × ChangeNOW hardware wallets comes to the booth, with ChangeNOW built in as the exclusive in-app swap provider. Owners swap assets across Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, EVM chains, and other supported networks inside the app, with no key exports and no outside services. Visitors can get a device by taking part in booth activities.

The zone also points forward: early-stage wallets that start working with ChangeNOW today can grow into this kind of collaboration as established players.

Merch

ChangeNOW merch at the booth includes pieces made with design brands such as Native Union. Quantities are limited.

Talks by ChangeNOW representatives

October 6, 11:30 AM, Origins Hackathon Opening

Pauline Shangett, strategic advisor at NOWNodes, opens the Origins hackathon at TOKEN2049.

October 7, 12:30 PM Bit Digital Stage

Pauline Shangett, CSO at ChangeNOW: Next Billion Users Should Never Touch Blockchain

Crypto set out to remove the middleman and removed the middleman's job with it. In place of one bank, people now hold an exchange, a wallet, a swap service, a bridge, a ramp, and a trading app, and the industry calls the result freedom. Shangett's argument is that the next billion users will not assemble a financial system out of parts, and that the fix is a product that takes the path while the user picks the result. The keynote lays out what ChangeNOW is building toward: a super app where one action starts where the last one ended.

October 8, 11:10 AM, Global Stage, HSC Singapore at the Fairmont

Martin Masser, Director of Strategic Partnerships at ChangeNOW: The Infrastructure Behind Liquidity

The topics on the table include how crypto infrastructure will carry the next financial system, the APAC playbook for regulatory clarity and commercial scale, and what a crypto super app has to get right. Speakers and times follow closer to the event.

Retrofuture Soiree by ChangeNOW: Singapore Edition

October 7, Welcome 8:00 PM, Singapore Time

After day one of the conference, ChangeNOW carries the booth's retrofuturist look into a bar: 1960s design and capsule architecture, custom cocktails, a cigar bar, late-night food, and a photo space. Every guest takes home a limited-edition item. Pauline Shangett and Martin Masser will host the evening.

The event is for ChangeNOW partners and people working in Web3. Admission is free and entry is by approved request only, with capacity limited; the organizers share the exact address with confirmed guests.

About ChangeNOW

ChangeNOW is a crypto super app built for every crypto move, giving newcomers, professionals, and businesses the tools they need to access Web3 finance in a simple and secure way.

ChangeNOW For Business applies the same stack to companies building crypto exchanges, wallets, payment gateways, and fiat ramps. It covers 1,500+ coins across 90+ networks and more than 2,250,000 exchange pairs, with liquidity sourced from CEXs and DEXs.
