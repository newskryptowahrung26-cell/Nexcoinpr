Vstock Transfer Expands Its Issuer Services Platform with Issuer-Sponsored Tokenized Shares Through a Strategic Collaboration with Metagood

Issuer: Vstock Transfer • Source: Decrypt → • Published: 23 November 2026

New York, United States, November 23rd, 2026

Public companies and companies preparing to go public will have a single agent for shareholder records, corporate actions and tokenized ownership as the SEC’s Innovation Exemption opens U.S. markets to onchain trading

Vstock Transfer, LLC (“Vstock”), an SEC-registered transfer agent and registrar serving approximately 1,000 issuers, including companies listed on the NYSE, Nasdaq and OTC Markets, today announced a strategic collaboration with Metagood, a tokenization and lifecycle management provider for regulated digital assets, under which it plans to add issuer-sponsored tokenized shares to its suite of issuer services.

Through the collaboration, Vstock will offer public companies and companies preparing to go public the ability to issue their shares in token form, supported by the same team that manages their shareholder records, corporate actions and annual meetings. Metagood’s patent-pending platform will connect directly to Vstock’s recordkeeping system, so token holders and book-entry holders will appear on a single shareholder register, and dividends, stock splits, tender offers and shareholder votes will be processed once for every holder.

The tokens will be the company’s own shares, recorded in token form by its transfer agent. They will not be wrappers, derivatives or synthetic products. They are designed so that a token holder is a registered shareholder with the same economic and voting rights as a holder of book-entry shares. The platform is interoperable across multiple blockchains and built to add more, so an issuer’s shares can be offered on the networks its investors use.

Tokenized shares will be the latest addition to Vstock’s expanding suite of services, which spans shareholder recordkeeping, DTC eligibility and DWAC transfers, corporate actions, proxy and annual meeting services, IPO and SPAC support, and equity administration. Vstock continues to build its platform around one principle: anticipating what issuers will need next and delivering it with the responsiveness required of the capital markets.

A Governance Decision, Not Just a Technology Decision

For boards and management teams, tokenization is now a governance question as much as a technology question. Sponsoring tokenized shares through their transfer agent gives companies a way to participate on their own terms, with their official register, their shareholder relationships and their corporate actions intact.

“Companies come to us for guidance well before they come to us for a service, and tokenization is now at the top of the list,” said Yoel Goldfeder, Chief Executive Officer of Vstock Transfer. “Issuers want to know how they can offer their shares in token form, and how to do it on their own terms. This collaboration gives them a clear path. We selected Metagood because its platform was built for what a transfer agent does every day after shares are issued: paying dividends, processing corporate actions and counting every vote accurately.”

“We built our first tokenization product at Vstock years ago, and we learned that the market was not ready for it,” said Seth Farbman, Chairman and Founder of Vstock Transfer. “The rules, the exchanges and investor demand have now caught up, and small and mid-cap companies deserve access to the same tools as the largest issuers. For a growth company CEO, tokenized shares can open the door to a new generation of investors and a more direct relationship with shareholders. Whether a company is preparing for its IPO, planning an uplisting or simply evaluating its options, we want Vstock to be its first call.”

Danny Yang, Chief Executive Officer of Metagood, commented, “Our platform will let Vstock handle every dividend, vote and corporate action for token holders with the same precision it delivers for book-entry holders today.”

Companies interested in issuer-sponsored tokenized shares, or in learning more about third-party tokenization notices under the Innovation Exemption, are invited to schedule a consultation with the Vstock team at info@vstocktransfer.com or visit www.vstocktransfer.com.

About Vstock Transfer

Vstock Transfer, LLC is an SEC-registered stock transfer agent and registrar serving approximately 1,000 issuers, from private companies and IPOs to public companies listed on the NYSE, NYSE American, Nasdaq and OTC Markets. Founded and managed by securities attorneys, Vstock provides shareholder recordkeeping, certificate services, DTC eligibility and DWAC transfers, proxy and annual meeting services, and equity administration tools. Vstock is based in New York.

For more information, visit www.vstocktransfer.com

About Metagood

Metagood provides next generation infrastructure for regulated financial assets. Its patent-pending platform connects traditional financial systems with blockchain networks to tokenize assets and automate their lifecycle, including transfers, payments, distributions and corporate actions. Built for financial institutions, transfer agents, exchanges and issuers, Metagood enables securities, mortgages and other regulated assets to be managed more efficiently and transparently across traditional and digital markets.

For more information, visit www.metagood.com
