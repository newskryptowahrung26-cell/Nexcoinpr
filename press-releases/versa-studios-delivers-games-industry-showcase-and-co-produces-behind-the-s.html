Versa Studios Delivers Games Industry Showcase and Co-Produces Behind-the-Scenes Production in Manchester

Leeds, United Kingdom, November 7th, 2026

Versa Studios has announced the delivery of a games-industry showcase and co-produced behind-the-scenes filming at Versa Studios Manchester, bringing together event support, studio facilities and specialist production services in a single project.

The showcase welcomed gamers and streamers from around the world to Manchester for an exclusive presentation of an upcoming game. Versa provided the studio environment for the event and supported its delivery, accommodating both the showcase and the production activity around it.

Alongside the event, Versa co-produced behind-the-scenes filming that included interviews with the game’s creators. The filming ran as a distinct strand of the project, and Versa’s involvement went beyond providing space to include coordinating people, equipment and production requirements across both the event and the filmed material.

A Multi-Disciplinary Studio Project

More than 40 people worked on the project. Versa coordinated crew, lighting, production design, sound and studio space, alongside other production requirements. Bringing these disciplines together in one studio meant the work covered both the physical environment for the showcase and the technical needs of the accompanying filming.

Virtual Production

Virtual production was a further component of the project. Versa brought in a specialist virtual production partner to construct a screen at Versa Studios Manchester and create immersive content inspired by the game. This added a screen-based environment to the production alongside the physical elements assembled for the showcase, and formed part of the wider production coordinated by Versa.

Manchester Capabilities

The project reflects the range of activity at Versa Studios Manchester, which includes virtual production, motion capture and wider production services. On this project, Versa’s role covered event support, production coordination and co-production of filmed material.

“This project shows how VERSA works as a production partner, bringing together people, technology, facilities and specialist partners across gaming, physical production and virtual production,” said Charlie Ingall, Executive Director of Versa Studios.

The assignment brought facilities, crew coordination, lighting, sound, production design and specialist support together within a single project, covering the event environment as well as the accompanying behind-the-scenes production.

About Versa Studios

VERSA Studios is a UK-wide film, television and broadcast studio network and production services partner to the film, television and games industries, with more than 600,000 sq ft of production space across London, Manchester and Leeds. Its facilities include premium sound stages, television studios, motion-capture and virtual production capabilities, fibre-linked broadcast galleries and end-to-end production services.

VERSA Studios is part of VERSA Media Group, an international media group developing intellectual property and services for the global media industry.

Credits include Sky’s Tinsel Town, Amazon Prime Video’s Maintenance Required, and the upcoming thriller Sirius, produced by VERSA’s production partner, Future Artists Entertainment. Other credits include Peaky Blinders and Marvel’s Secret Wars. Entertainment credits include ITV’s Bullseye and Cooking with the Stars, Netflix’s Building the Band and RuPaul’s Drag Race UK. VERSA is also home to Big Brother, Dragons’ Den, BBC Morning Live and Blue Peter.
