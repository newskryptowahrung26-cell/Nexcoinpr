PrimeXBT Launches Anthropic Pre-IPO Crypto Futures, Bringing New Trading Opportunities in AI

Castries, Saint Lucia, November 17th, 2026 PrimeXBT , a global multi-asset broker and crypto asset service provider, has announced the...

Issuer: PrimeXBT • Source: Decrypt → • Published: 17 November 2026

Commercial Content Disclosure: The following announcement is an official press release syndicated from Decrypt on behalf of PrimeXBT. NexcoinPR provides media distribution and editorial hosting. This content does not represent independent editorial reporting or investment advice.

Castries, Saint Lucia, November 17th, 2026

PrimeXBT, a global multi-asset broker and crypto asset service provider, has announced the launch of Pre-IPO Trading, a new category within its Crypto Futures offering, starting with ANT/USDT*. The new instrument gives traders an opportunity to trade the market-implied pre-IPO valuation of Anthropic, the company behind Claude and one of the most anticipated listings of the year, before it goes public.

ANT/USDT is a USDT-margined perpetual Crypto Futures contract available on PrimeXBT's native PXTrader 2.0 platform. Traders can open long or short positions, trade 24/7, including weekends, and use use of up to 1:5, with a minimum order size of 0.01 contract. Prices are determined through a live order book, reflecting market expectations rather than an official company valuation. As part of PrimeXBT's Crypto Futures, Pre-IPO Trading comes with competitive fees of 0.01% for makers and from 0.045% for takers. 

Anthropic is expected to begin trading on Nasdaq on 19 November under the ticker ANT, with an indicative IPO price range of $70, $78 per share. Upon listing, PrimeXBT plans to transition ANT/USDT into a standard perpetual Crypto Futures contract linked to the listed stock. If the IPO is cancelled, the contract will be delisted, and open positions may be closed or settled at a reference price, with advance notice to clients. The contract does not convert into actual shares.

Jonatan Randin, CMT, Senior Market Analyst at PrimeXBT commented: "AI is driving some of the most significant developments in financial markets, with Anthropic at the centre of that momentum. Pre-IPO Trading gives our clients a new way to participate in major market trends, making emerging trading opportunities more accessible through innovative technology and competitive trading conditions.”

The launch builds on PrimeXBT's offering of over 170 Crypto Futures markets, including contracts linked to major technology companies such as Tesla, NVIDIA, SpaceX, Circle and Robinhood. Clients can access Pre-IPO Trading alongside cryptocurrencies and CFDs through one PrimeXBT account, with fiat and crypto-denominated account options in USDT, USDC, BTC and ETH. This allows traders to stay crypto-native and access multiple markets without converting their holdings into fiat or funding a separate brokerage account.

With the launch of Anthropic Pre-IPO Trading, PrimeXBT further strengthens its position as a leading multi-asset trading provider, bridging crypto and traditional financial markets together and expanding trading possibilities across AI, technology and equity markets.

To start trading Anthropic Pre-IPO Crypto Futures, users can visit PrimeXBT website.

*ANT/USDT does not confer ownership of or any rights in Anthropic shares and is not issued, sponsored or endorsed by Anthropic. Prices are based on an assumed 13.1bln shares and may differ materially from any official valuation or IPO price. The instrument may be converted, settled or delisted upon IPO or IPO cancellation, including forced closure of open positions. Funding fees apply.
