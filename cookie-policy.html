1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet, or other device) by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to deliver information to website operators about how their site is being used.

Cookies are not harmful programs. They cannot run code or deliver viruses. A cookie simply stores a small amount of data, often just a reference ID, on your device. Most cookies are session-based and are automatically deleted when you close your browser. Others are persistent and remain on your device for a set period.

Similar technologies, such as web beacons, pixel tags, and local storage, can function in a similar way to cookies. References to "cookies" in this policy include these similar technologies where applicable.

2. How We Use Cookies

NexcoinPR uses cookies and similar technologies for the following general purposes:

  • To ensure the website functions correctly and reliably.
  • To remember your cookie consent preferences so we do not repeatedly ask for your consent.
  • To understand how visitors interact with our website so that we can improve its content, performance, and usability.
  • To allow certain website features and functionality.

We do not use cookies to track you across third-party websites or to serve you personalised advertising.

NexcoinPR uses the following categories of cookies:

4. Necessary Cookies

Necessary cookies (sometimes called "strictly necessary" cookies) are required for the main functionality of the NexcoinPR website. Without these cookies, certain features of the website cannot operate correctly.

Examples of what necessary cookies do on the NexcoinPR website:

  • Maintaining session continuity as you navigate between pages.
  • Enabling secure connections and standard security features.
  • Supporting form functionality so that contact and submission forms work correctly.

Legal basis: Necessary cookies do not require your consent as they are strictly necessary for the website to function. They are placed automatically when you visit the website.

Can you block them? You can block necessary cookies through your browser settings, but doing so may cause parts of the website to not function correctly.

5. Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the NexcoinPR website. They collect information about which pages are visited, how long visitors spend on pages, where visitors come from, and which content is most popular. This helps us improve the website's content, structure, and interface usability.

Analytics data collected via cookies is aggregated and used to identify trends rather than to track individual users. Where analytics tools are in use, IP addresses are anonymised where technically possible.

Legal basis: Analytics cookies are non-necessary and require your consent before being placed. You will be asked for consent via the cookie banner when you first visit the website.

Can you block them? Yes. You can decline analytics cookies via the cookie consent banner or through your browser settings. Declining analytics cookies will not affect your ability to use the website, but it will mean we receive less information about how you use the site.

6. Preference Cookies

Preference cookies are used to remember choices you make when visiting the website. On NexcoinPR, preference cookies are primarily used to:

  • Store your cookie consent preference (whether you have accepted or rejected non-necessary cookies), so that the cookie consent banner is not displayed repeatedly during subsequent visits.

Legal basis: The cookie that stores your consent preference is technically necessary, it is necessary to record your decision so that we can honour it. It is placed when you make a choice via the cookie consent banner.

7. Cookie Consent

When you first visit the NexcoinPR website, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner. This banner allows you to:

  • Accept all cookies, including analytics cookies.
  • Reject non-necessary cookies, including analytics cookies.

Necessary cookies are placed regardless of your choice, as they are necessary for the website to function.

Your consent preference is stored in a cookie on your device and will be remembered for up to 12 months. After this period, you may be asked for your consent again.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the website, at which point the consent banner will be displayed again.

8. Managing Your Cookies

You have several options for managing cookies:

  • Via the cookie consent banner: When you first visit NexcoinPR, use the cookie consent banner to accept or reject non-necessary cookies. Your choice will be remembered for future visits.
  • Via your browser settings: All major web browsers allow you to control cookies through the browser settings. See Section 9 for guidance on common browsers.
  • Clearing cookies: You can delete all cookies stored on your device through your browser settings at any time. Be aware that deleting cookies will also clear any stored preferences, including your cookie consent preference, and may log you out of websites where you are signed in.

9. Managing Cookies via Browser Settings

You can manage, block, or delete cookies through your browser's settings. The process varies by browser:

Google Chrome

Go to Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data. From here you can block third-party cookies, clear existing cookies, or manage exceptions for specific sites.

Mozilla Firefox

Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data. Firefox allows you to choose a level of protection (Standard, Strict, or Custom) and manage individual cookies.

Apple Safari

Go to Preferences (or Settings on iOS) → Privacy. Safari allows you to block all cookies, or only third-party cookies, and to manage website data individually.

Microsoft Edge

Go to Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data. Edge allows you to block third-party cookies, manage stored cookies, and configure site-specific cookie settings.

Opera

Go to Settings → Advanced → Privacy & Security → Site Settings → Cookies and site data. Opera's cookie controls are similar to those in Chrome.

For instructions on other browsers, consult your browser's help documentation. Note that blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of the NexcoinPR website and other websites you visit.

10. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies placed on the NexcoinPR website may be set by third-party services that we use, such as analytics providers. These third-party cookies are subject to the privacy and cookie policies of those third parties, and NexcoinPR does not control them directly.

Where third-party analytics cookies are in use, they are only placed with your consent (except where they are strictly necessary to a service we use). We select third-party providers carefully and only work with those who provide appropriate data protection commitments.

NexcoinPR does not use third-party advertising networks or social media tracking cookies that monitor your activity across other websites for the purpose of serving you targeted advertising.

11. Changes to This Cookie Policy

NexcoinPR may update this Cookie Policy from time to time as our use of cookies changes or as legal requirements evolve. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date on this page.

Where changes are major, we may also reset cookie consent preferences and ask you to confirm your preferences again. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
