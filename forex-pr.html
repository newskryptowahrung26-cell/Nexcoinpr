Institutional Compliance

The Forex PR Regulatory Compliance Framework (FCA, CySEC, ASIC)

Forex public relations requires strict adherence to global financial promotions rules. We protect your brokerage's licenses by enforcing rigorous compliance protocols:

🇬🇧

UK FCA Financial Promotions Rules

Mandatory statutory risk warnings, clear disclosure of leverage limits (maximum 1:30 for retail under ESMA/FCA rules), negative balance protection disclosures, and avoidance of any inducements to trade.

🇨🇾

CySEC & European MiFID II Standards

Adherence to cross-border European passporting rules, clear identification of the operating legal entity, standardized client fund segregation notices, and prohibition of misleading bonus promotions.

🇦🇺

ASIC & Global Frameworks

Compliance with Australian Product Design and Distribution Obligations (DDO), Target Market Determinations (TMD), and offshore regulatory entity disclosures (FSCA, FSA, FSC).

Mandatory Statutory Financial Risk Disclaimer

CFDs and foreign exchange products are complex instruments and come with a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage. Between 74% and 89% of retail investor accounts lose money when trading CFDs. You should consider whether you understand how CFDs work and whether you can afford to take the high risk of losing your money. NexcoinPR does not provide investment or financial advice.

Content Standards

Content Requirements for Forex Clients

To ensure high media pickup and protect our publisher relationships, all forex press releases must satisfy our editorial checklist:

  • Substantive News Hook: Licensing milestone, platform upgrade, audited payout report, or research launch.
  • Identifiable Operating Entity: Verifiable corporate registration number, jurisdiction, and official regulatory license numbers.
  • Mandatory Risk Disclosures: Standardized risk warning disclaiming investment advice and disclosing retail CFD risk statistics.
  • Authentic Executive Quotes: Meaningful commentary from verified leadership or chief market strategists.
Prohibited Content

Prohibited Forex Claims

The following content will be rejected immediately:

  • Trading signal promises, automated bot return guarantees, or "no-risk" claims.
  • Unregulated offshore entities claiming to hold Tier-1 licenses without verifiable registry proof.
  • Misleading trading bonuses or deposit matching schemes violating ESMA/FCA rules.
  • Unverified payout claims or fake screenshots from prop trading operations.
