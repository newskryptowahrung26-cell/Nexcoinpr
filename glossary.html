Terminology & Concepts

Crypto, Forex & PR Industry Glossary

Clear, factual definitions for main concepts across digital assets, blockchain engineering, currency trading, and public relations.

Layer 2 (L2) Rollup

A supporting framework or protocol built on top of a base blockchain (such as Ethereum) to improve transaction capacity and reduce transaction fees by executing transactions off-chain and posting cryptographic verification proofs back to Layer 1.

Category: Blockchain • Related: Blockchain PR

Zero-Knowledge (ZK) Proof

A cryptographic method enabling one party (the prover) to prove to another party (the verifier) that a statement is true without revealing any information beyond the statement's validity. Necessary for blockchain scaling and privacy preservation.

Category: Cryptography • Related: Blockchain PR

DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure)

Decentralized networks using blockchain protocols and token incentives to deploy physical hardware infrastructure, such as wireless networks, compute clusters, sensor grids, and energy storage systems.

Category: Web3 • Related: Web3 PR

RWA (Real-World Assets)

Tangible or traditional financial assets, such as real estate, government bonds, commodities, and credit facilities, tokenized on a blockchain to allow fractional ownership, 24/7 liquidity, and automated on-chain settlement.

Category: Institutional Crypto • Related: Financial PR

Token Generation Event (TGE)

The formal technical and commercial milestone in which a blockchain protocol mints and distributes its cryptographic tokens to foundation treasuries, early backers, liquidity pools, and community members.

Category: Token Launches • Related: Crypto PR

Airdrop

The distribution of cryptocurrency tokens or coins to wallet addresses belonging to active community members, testnet participants, or early protocol users to decentralize governance and bootstrap adoption.

Category: Community • Related: Web3 PR

Prop Trading Firm (Proprietary Trading)

A financial firm that provides funded trading capital to skilled retail traders who pass simulated evaluation challenges, sharing profits generated in financial markets including forex, commodities, and digital assets.

Category: Forex & Trading • Related: Forex PR

CFD (Contract for Difference)

A derivative trading instrument allowing market participants to speculate on price movements of underlying assets (currencies, crypto, equities) without physically owning the asset.

Category: Financial Trading • Related: Forex PR

Crypto Newswire

A dedicated news distribution and syndication service engineered to deliver corporate announcements, product releases, and market news directly to cryptocurrency journalists, trading desks, and search engine indexes.

Category: Media • Related: Press Release Distribution

Earned Media

Organic publicity, editorial news coverage, interviews, and brand mentions generated through public relations and journalistic merit rather than direct advertising spend.

Category: Public Relations • Related: Crypto PR Guide

Bitcoin (BTC)

The first decentralized digital currency, introduced in 2008 by an anonymous programmer or group known as Satoshi Nakamoto. Bitcoin operates on a proof-of-work peer-to-peer network without central authority intervention.

Category: Cryptocurrency • Related: Crypto News

Blockchain

A distributed, immutable digital ledger that cryptographically records transactions across a decentralized network of nodes. Each block contains a cryptographic hash of the previous block, timestamp, and transaction data.

Category: Technology • Related: Blockchain PR

Boilerplate (PR)

A standardized paragraph positioned at the end of a press release providing concise background on the issuing organization, its founding date, mission, leadership, and official website URL.

Category: Public Relations • Related: Writing Guide

Central Bank

A national or supra-national financial institution (such as the Federal Reserve, ECB, or Bank of England) responsible for setting monetary policy, controlling currency supply, and setting benchmark interest rates.

Category: Financial Markets • Related: Forex News

Crypto PR

Strategic communications and media relations dedicated to cryptocurrency companies, Layer 1/2 protocols, and tokenized platforms to secure earned media coverage and build verified industry reputation.

Category: Public Relations • Related: Crypto PR Services

DeFi (Decentralized Finance)

An ecosystem of financial applications built atop blockchain smart contracts that recreate traditional banking, lending, and exchange services without central intermediaries.

Category: Web3 • Related: Web3 PR

Ethereum

An open-source, globally decentralized blockchain platform supporting programmable smart contracts and decentralized applications (dApps), powered by its native asset Ether (ETH).

Category: Blockchain • Related: Crypto News

Forex (Foreign Exchange)

The decentralized global marketplace for exchanging national currencies against one another. It is the largest financial market globally by daily trading volume.

Category: Financial Markets • Related: Forex PR Services

Pip (Percentage in Point)

The smallest standardized unit of price movement in foreign exchange markets, typically equivalent to 0.0001 for most major currency pairs.

Category: Forex Trading

Press Release

An official, factual written announcement issued to journalists and media outlets reporting a verifiable new development, product launch, corporate appointment, or strategic event.

Category: Media • Related: Distribution Platform

Smart Contract

Self-executing code stored on a blockchain ledger that autonomously enforces the terms of an agreement when predefined conditions are cryptographically met.

Category: Blockchain Technology

Web3

An evolving vision of the World Wide Web built on decentralized protocols, cryptographic tokens, and user-owned data and sovereign identity systems.

Category: Technology • Related: Web3 PR Services
