Editorial Policy

Our standards for independence, accuracy, transparency, and accountability in all content published on NexcoinPR.

1. Editorial Independence

NexcoinPR operates a dual function: we are a PR and media distribution agency for clients, and we also publish independent editorial content covering the cryptocurrency, forex, blockchain, Web3, and fintech industries. These two functions are kept operationally and editorially separate.

Our editorial team makes all decisions about which topics to cover, which sources to cite, and how to frame stories based solely on editorial merit and public interest. Commercial clients, advertising partners, and press release submitters have no influence over editorial content that is produced independently by NexcoinPR's editorial team.

No individual, company, or commercial partner can pay to receive favourable editorial coverage, improved editorial rankings, or any form of editorial endorsement. Coverage decisions are made independently of commercial relationships.

2. Content Type Labels

To ensure full transparency, every piece of content published on NexcoinPR is clearly labelled with a content type. Readers should always be able to identify the origin and nature of any content they read on our platform. The following content type labels are used:

Editorial

Independent journalism produced by NexcoinPR's own editorial team. Editorial content reflects the independent judgement of the author and is not influenced by commercial relationships. It is subject to all accuracy, fact-checking, and editorial standards described in this policy.

News

News reporting by NexcoinPR's editorial team covering developments in the crypto, forex, blockchain, Web3, and fintech sectors. News content is reported factually with named or attributed sources wherever possible and is independent of commercial influence.

Press Release

Content submitted by companies for distribution via NexcoinPR's press release distribution service. Press releases are written and provided by the submitting company. NexcoinPR does not independently verify the claims made within press releases. They do not represent the editorial views or endorsement of NexcoinPR. The submitting company is solely responsible for the accuracy of the content.

Sponsored

Paid commercial content produced by or on behalf of a paying client. Sponsored content is clearly distinguished from independent editorial content at all times. Readers should understand that sponsored content represents the views of the paying partner, not the independent editorial views of NexcoinPR.

Partner Content

Content produced in collaboration with a brand partner. Partner content may involve co-production or contribution from an external brand and will always be clearly identified. Like sponsored content, partner content does not represent the independent editorial views of NexcoinPR.

3. Accuracy Standards

NexcoinPR is committed to publishing editorial content that is accurate, fair, and based on the best available information at the time of publication. Our editorial standards require that:

  • All factual claims in editorial and news content are supported by credible, verifiable sources.
  • Prices, figures, and market data are sourced from reputable data providers and are accurate at the time of writing.
  • Quotes attributed to individuals are accurate and contextually fair.
  • Where information is subject to uncertainty or ongoing development, this is clearly indicated in the text.
  • Opinions and analysis are clearly distinguished from reported facts.
  • We do not knowingly publish false or misleading information.

Because financial markets, regulatory environments, and technology developments change rapidly, some information may become outdated after publication. Where we become aware of significant changes that affect the accuracy of published content, we will update or annotate the relevant article.

4. Corrections Process

NexcoinPR takes errors seriously. If we publish inaccurate information in editorial or news content, we are committed to correcting it promptly and transparently. Full details of how errors are handled are set out in our Corrections Policy.

To report an error, contact our editorial team at editorial@nexcoinpr.com. We will acknowledge all valid error reports and endeavour to respond within five business days.

5. Source Citation Standards

Editorial and news content published by NexcoinPR follows clear source citation standards to ensure readers can verify information and assess its credibility:

  • Primary sources — Original documents, official announcements, company filings, regulatory publications, and on-the-record interviews are preferred and cited by name where possible.
  • Secondary sources — Where primary sources are unavailable, reputable secondary sources such as established financial news organisations, academic institutions, and industry research bodies are used and attributed.
  • Data sources — Market data, price information, and statistical claims are attributed to their source (for example, CoinGecko, TradingView, or official exchange data).
  • Anonymous sources — NexcoinPR uses anonymous sourcing only in exceptional circumstances where information is significant and the source cannot be named for legitimate reasons. Anonymous sources are described in general terms so readers can assess credibility.
  • Hyperlinks — External links are used to direct readers to source material where available online.

6. Fact-Checking Approach

All editorial and news content published by NexcoinPR goes through an editorial review process before publication. This process includes:

  • Verification of key factual claims against primary or reputable secondary sources.
  • Review of quoted figures, dates, and names for accuracy.
  • Editorial review by a senior member of the editorial team.
  • Cross-referencing claims against official sources (regulatory filings, company announcements, etc.) where applicable.

We do not apply independent fact-checking to press releases or sponsored content. Press releases are the sole responsibility of the submitting company. Sponsored content is clearly labelled and produced under commercial arrangements.

7. Author Accountability

Editorial and news content published on NexcoinPR is attributed to named authors wherever possible. Each author is responsible for the accuracy and quality of their work, subject to editorial oversight.

Author profiles may be published on the NexcoinPR website and may include biographical information and areas of expertise. Authors are required to disclose to the editorial team any personal financial interests that could represent a conflict of interest in relation to content they produce.

Where content is produced by the NexcoinPR editorial team collectively without a single named author, it will be attributed to "NexcoinPR Editorial Team" or "NexcoinPR Staff".

8. Financial Content Standards

NexcoinPR covers cryptocurrency, forex, financial markets, and related topics. All editorial and news content involving financial matters is published strictly for informational purposes. NexcoinPR does not provide financial advice, investment advice, trading advice, or any recommendations to buy, sell, or hold any asset, currency, or financial instrument.

All content covering financial topics includes appropriate disclaimers to this effect. Readers are advised to conduct their own research and consult a qualified financial adviser before making any investment or trading decisions. Past performance of any asset class does not guarantee or indicate future results.

9. Rankings and Media Placements

NexcoinPR does not guarantee specific editorial rankings, search engine positions, or media placements for any client, press release, or commercial partner. Where editorial rankings or "best of" lists are produced, these represent the independent editorial judgement of NexcoinPR's editorial team at the time of publication and are not influenced by commercial relationships or financial consideration.

Rankings may be updated as market conditions, products, and services evolve.

10. Conflicts of Interest Policy

NexcoinPR operates in the crypto and financial PR industry, which means commercial relationships with clients exist alongside editorial publishing. To manage potential conflicts of interest:

  • Commercial clients are not given favourable editorial coverage as a result of their client status.
  • Authors and editorial team members are required to disclose any personal financial holdings in assets, projects, or companies they cover editorially.
  • Where a potential conflict of interest exists, it is disclosed within the relevant article or the author is not assigned to cover that topic.
  • Advertising and commercial partnerships do not influence editorial decisions.
  • Press releases and sponsored content are clearly labelled and treated as separate from editorial content at all times.

11. Contact the Editorial Team

For editorial enquiries, corrections, content concerns, or to report an error, please contact our editorial team:

Email: editorial@nexcoinpr.com

We aim to acknowledge all editorial enquiries within two business days.
