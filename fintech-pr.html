Specialized Practice Areas

The 5 Core Fintech PR Pillars & Tactical Playbooks

From venture funding announcements to enterprise core banking integrations, our playbooks build enduring institutional credibility.

Pillar 1 — Global Settlement

Payment Rails & Cross-Border Settlement PR

Modern payment infrastructure requires positioning that speaks simultaneously to enterprise Chief Technology Officers, merchant aggregators, and institutional banking partners.

  • Real-Time Payment Network Integrations: Media campaigns highlighting integrations with FedNow, SEPA Instant, Pix, UPI, and Faster Payments.
  • Stablecoin Payment Infrastructure: Positioning enterprise settlement on USDC/USDT rails for cross-border B2B trade and treasury optimization.
  • Merchant Acquiring & Checkout Optimization: Highlighting payment conversion uplift, multi-currency routing, and biometric authentication.
  • ISO 20022 Migration Milestones: Communicating rich financial messaging compliance for tier-1 banking procurement.
Payments Playbook Outcome: Inbound enterprise merchant inquiries, streamlined bank sponsorship approvals, and high-DA backlink authority.
Pillar 2 — Digital Banking

Neobanks, Digital Banking & BaaS PR

Consumer and business neobanks must demonstrate solvency, technological agility, and deposit safety to attract retail depositors and corporate accounts.

  • Deposit & Customer Milestones: Announcing 100K, 1M, and 5M account milestones with verifiable growth metrics to establish market leadership.
  • Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS) Integrations: Promoting white-label account, debit card, and credit underwriting capabilities to non-fintech enterprise brands.
  • SME Credit & Working Capital Solutions: Framing automated revenue-based financing and AI credit scoring for business publications.
  • FDIC / Statutory Insurance Safeguards: Clearly communicating pass-through deposit insurance partnerships to assure depositor confidence.
Digital Banking Outcome: Accelerated account acquisition, reduced customer acquisition cost (CAC), and trusted brand reputation.
Pillar 3 — Compliance Tech

Regtech, AML & Compliance Software PR

Regulatory technology software providers must convince institutional Chief Risk Officers (CROs) of their technical rigor and auditability.

  • Automated KYC/KYB & Identity Verification: Press releases framing synthetic identity fraud prevention and liveness detection algorithms.
  • Transaction Surveillance & AML Engines: Showcasing false positive reduction, real-time blockchain analytics, and sanction screening.
  • Regulatory Change Management Software: Positioning automated regulatory reporting tools ahead of new compliance deadlines.
  • Enterprise Bank Procurement Clearance: Showcasing SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 certifications, and formal enterprise security validation.
Regtech Playbook Outcome: Elevated institutional procurement trust and accelerated sales cycles with Fortune 500 banks.
Pillar 4 — Wealth & Investment

Wealthtech, Robo-Advisory & Investment Rails PR

Algorithmic wealth management, fractional investing platforms, and automated retirement software require financial press credibility to win high-net-worth trust.

  • Assets Under Management (AUM) Milestones: Announcing institutional AUM benchmarks and automated rebalancing performance data.
  • Fractional Share & Alternative Asset Platforms: Positioning fractional equities, art, real estate, and private market investment access.
  • Brokerage API Integrations: Media coverage for zero-commission clearing rails and embedded investing SDKs for third-party apps.
  • Chief Investment Officer (CIO) Thought Leadership: Syndicating macroeconomic market outlooks and asset allocation strategies to financial desks.
Wealthtech Outcome: High-net-worth depositor acquisition, B2B RIA channel expansion, and sustained financial press citations.
Pillar 5 — Venture Capital

Enterprise Integrity

Fintech Regulatory Compliance & Standards

Financial technology communications operate under strict regulatory and banking scrutiny. Our compliance-first editorial framework safeguards your corporate reputation:

  • Banking & Card Network Alignment: Strict adherence to Visa, Mastercard, and partner bank branding and disclosure requirements.
  • Accurate Regulatory Status: Clear differentiation between licensed banking entities and non-bank technology service providers (BaaS models).
  • Factual Security Claims: Verification of SOC 2, PCI-DSS, ISO 27001, and encryption standards before public dissemination.
  • Non-Promotional Investment Disclaimers: Standardized disclosures ensuring content is never construed as investment advice.
Prohibited Content

Prohibited Claims

The following content will be rejected immediately:

  • False claims of holding a direct banking charter when operating via sponsor banks.
  • Guaranteed investment yields, trading profit promises, or speculative returns.
  • Unverified customer numbers, transaction volumes, or revenue statistics.
  • Plagiarized whitepaper sections or duplicate software claims.
