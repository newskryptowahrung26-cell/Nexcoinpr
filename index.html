How Press Release Distribution Works

A straightforward four-step process from submission to publication and reporting.

1

Submit Your Content

Send your press release or brief to our editorial team for review via our online submission form or email.

2

Editorial Review

Our editors review content for accuracy, clarity and compliance with our editorial standards before approving for distribution.

3

Distribution

Your press release is distributed across financial and crypto media channels appropriate to your sector and selected package.

4

Reporting

Receive a distribution report detailing where your press release was published, providing transparency on your campaign's reach.
