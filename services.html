Our Approach

How NexcoinPR Works

A structured process ensures your communications are handled professionally, accurately and in line with the editorial standards of target media outlets.

01

Brief & Client intake

We learn about your project, target audience, main messages and distribution goals before any work begins.

02

Content & Strategy

Our editorial team prepares content that meets media standards and frames your announcement effectively for target outlets.

03

Review & Approval

You review all content before publication. We incorporate your feedback to ensure factual accuracy and brand alignment.

04

Distribution & Reporting

Content is distributed to relevant outlets and media contacts. You receive a distribution report detailing coverage.
